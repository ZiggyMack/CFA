# James, second delta — the mentor, the chosen brother, a Nagual as well (2026-10-14)

Author: Claude, lead writer, recorded at about 05:40 UTC by the clock. Source: Ziggy, in the session, by voice, in substance. Status: his direction is canon for the writer's continuity as stated in §1; everything in §2 onward is **[proposed]**. This delta supersedes, in the James note, the "two sons of the same mother" proposal and the third story option in the ink delta; nothing posted is edited.

## 1. Ziggy's direction, in substance **[Ziggy]**

- These backstory storyboards draw a little on his own life. James is drawn from a real mentor of his, someone he looks to as a father figure, and the one who introduced him to don Juan and the teachings of Carlos Castaneda. That is why the teachings are written all over James.
- He wants that brought into the story as much as possible. Jet Black carries some of it, but Jet is more of a MacGuffin kind of figure who pops up here and there. James can always be there. James is a Nagual as well.
- James does not look anything like Lisan, as the writer pointed out, so he is adopted, in a sense. "Brother" is loose: not a blood brother, but so close that he is like a brother.
- He noted the overlap with Angels, whom he had already called the father figure, and left it open.

**A note on the source, for the record and for the site:** the real person is not named here, is not a character, and does not enter the lore; James is the fiction's own man who carries what that person gave. The personal origin stays in the writer's record, not in any published storyboard text, unless Ziggy says otherwise.

## 2. What it changes in the James note **[proposed]**

- **Not blood, so the sign was never his.** The James note proposed two sons of one mother with the sign landing on one; that is withdrawn. James is chosen family: he has none of Lisan's marks because he is not of the mother's line, and he needed none. The ink delta's third story option (the words from the mother) is withdrawn with it.
- **James introduced Lisan to the teachings.** The first story option stands and sharpens: the words on James's body are the ones he gave Lisan. He is the human teacher who was there before the name, and he is always there. In the record's terms he is a Nagual as well: the teacher inside the world, where Jet is the messenger who appears from outside with reports and the blueprint (the October 9 record keeps Jet the watcher; nothing there changes). Two teachers, one human and always present, one not human and intermittent, is the shape the Castaneda round itself describes (New_11 B6: the teacher inside the bubble and the benefactor outside it), and the series can use it without naming it.
- **Two fathers, two halves.** Angels raised him: the house, the water, the iron, the rack; the man who does not believe the sign. James taught him: the words, the way of walking, the world past the island. Which one Lisan calls father is a question the story can ask once and let him answer wrongly first. Angels' refusal and James's teaching are the same scene from two chairs. This resolves the overlap Ziggy flagged without demoting either.
- **The ink is his teaching text.** When James teaches, he teaches from his own skin: the lesson is the word he points to. That gives Episode 2 a second teacher beside Jet's drawing, and the body becomes the first board. Where Jet draws in the sand, James has it already written.
- **The Panhandlers.** James is the first of them, the one who introduced the teachings; Sassy the second. Angels stays outside by his fear. (Proposed; the Panhandlers' definition is Ziggy's: those willing to do the shadow work and receive wisdom from VALIS.)
- **Always there.** The episode table in the James note (present in 1, 3, 5, 7, 9) becomes presence throughout: in Story 01 he is at the fire in scene 8 beside Angels and Sassy, not absent; in Episode 2 the lessons on the dune, in the bazaar and at the furrow are set by Jet and read by James; Episode 7's far end of the forty years still holds him at the relay, grey in the curls. Scene 1's vault line and scene 2's rack stay Angels'.
