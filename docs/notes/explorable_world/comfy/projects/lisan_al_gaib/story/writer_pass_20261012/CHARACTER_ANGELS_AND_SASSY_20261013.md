# Character note — Angels and Sassy, the older couple who stay (preliminary, 2026-10-13)

Author: Claude, lead writer, about 03:40 UTC, from three spoken notes Ziggy gave in the session at about 03:05, 03:12 and 03:30 UTC. He said of the first, "don't quote me on those exact specifics," so his direction is recorded in substance, not as quotation, and everything I add is labeled **[proposed]**. Status: two characters to develop, on Ziggy's word that the series needs them even where nothing emphasises them. No image, request, voice or generation comes from this file; the coverage call bars a set for either until this note is read and looks exist (COVERAGE_CALL_20261013.md rule 7). Both names come from the song itself, so they are ours.

## 1. What Ziggy gave (in substance)

**Angels.**
- **The name is from the song and stays Angels.** The last line of Verse 1 is "Mirrors cook don't you know? Who cares? Angel knows-" (user text; panel 22 in the cue map). The lyric prints "Angel"; Ziggy wants his name kept as **Angels**. The cue keeps the lyric's spelling; the character is Angels.
- **A bound pair with Lisan.** Two bound together, the way Ziggy's shorthand put it (he reached for a franchise image of two joined in one power; the note keeps only the idea). Angels is not the greater power.
- **The Brutus.** The bestie who follows and might betray. We are not sure he will. He is following Lisan.
- **Recurring, so developed.** Even with no emphasis on him, the character has to exist, which means he has to be built.
- **Old.** Older than Lisan. A father figure, because Lisan's mother is dead (Story 01, scene 7: "One of the three was my mother"); Lisan looks to this older man as if he were his father. Lisan's father is not in the record either way.
- **The relationship is older than the name.** It was the established relationship before Lisan became Lisan Al Gaib. Once Lisan is on mission, Angels is the surrogate father who follows him.
- **The tension.** Angels does not believe in the prophecies of Lisan Al Gaib. He does understand that Lisan has great power. Ziggy's emphasis: there has to be tension between them.

**Sassy.**
- **The name is from the song and is a nickname.** Verse 1: "Sassy Frass, yeah she's a bitch - / Baddest bitch, in the mirror - with respect love endearing… but she's the queen - no mistake she is the qwang …. Salute, mama - auf Wiedersehen" (user text; panels 15 "baddest in the mirror" and 18 "salute mama" in the cue map). Ziggy: "Sassy" is more of a nickname; label her that way for our purposes; whether the videos ever call her Sassy is undecided.
- **The archetypal mother.** The stand-in for the mother to Lisan, though his own mother is dead.
- **The people in the rubble.** She and Angels are the people in the dust who are sticking it out with Lisan.
- **An item.** Sassy and Angels are very much a couple.

## 2. The reading (why the song already has them)

"Who cares? Angel knows" is the song's statement of the father: the young man shrugs the question off, and the old man who does not believe in him is the one who knows what it costs. "She's the queen… Salute, mama - auf Wiedersehen" is the song's statement of the mother: the one he salutes and says goodbye to in the same breath, because the mother he had is gone and the one he has is borrowed. Angels is the knowing without the faith. Sassy is the faith without the knowing. Between them they are the two halves of a parent, and the boy between them is the one with the marks.

## 3. Who they are **[proposed, for Ziggy's reading]**

- **In the settlement:** Angels keeps the rack (Story 01, scene 2: the iron thumpers "used by the Keepers' fathers and by nobody now"). He made or mended them; he knows the open sand as craft, not as sign; he has seen the worm and buried people who went out. Sassy keeps the fire the camp eats from, and she is the one the women bring the cups to when the Keepers' measure is short. Neither is a Keeper; neither wears a mark; the Keepers tolerate them because a rack nobody uses and a fire everybody needs are both harmless.
- **Their past with the knowing:** a generation ago, when the man with half the blueprint opened the scoop in the storm and three died, Angels carried the bodies up, and Sassy washed them. One of the three was Lisan's mother, who was Sassy's friend. They have raised the boy since, in the plain way of two people who did not ask to, and Angels has never once called the marks on the boy's throat anything but ink. Sassy has never once called them ink. **[proposed; it gives him a reason to distrust anyone who says "I know," gives her a reason to believe, and puts both of them exactly where Lisan is.]**
- **What each believes:** Angels believes in water, iron, weather and the worm. Not the dead sign, not a voice from outside, not a man with no face. He has watched Lisan do things nobody else can (the rise, the valve) and he does not call it prophecy; he calls it the boy, and he is afraid of what the boy is for. Sassy believes the boy is what his mother said he was, and she is afraid of the same thing from the other side: that he is for it.
- **The couple's own tension:** she keeps Angels following when he would stop; he keeps her from kneeling when she would. Every argument they have is the series' argument in a kitchen. **[proposed]**
- **The loaded gun (the Brutus seed):** the Keepers know Angels raised him. When they want Lisan brought back, or stopped, Angels is the one they ask, and he does not say no. Whether he ever does it is left open for the whole series. **[proposed mechanism; Ziggy's "we're not even sure if he's gonna betray him" is kept as written: the gun is loaded and the plan never fires it.]** The alternative, if Ziggy prefers the betrayal nearer: Angels believes Jet is the thing that will get Lisan killed, and would trade Jet to the Keepers to keep the boy; that is a betrayal of the mission, not of the son, and it is the kind he could live with. Sassy would never forgive it, which is the other reason he has not done it.
- **How they see Jet:** Angels sees a man with no face teaching his boy to be quiet; he never addresses Jet. Sassy sets a cup at the edge of the firelight for him every night, and every morning it is full. **[proposed; Jet does not drink, and she keeps doing it.]**
- **The one thing Angels can always do:** recognise Lisan. In the stalking lesson (Seed 08: move through the market as the market, be remembered by no one), the market forgets Lisan and Angels does not, because he raised him. Jet marks the lesson passed; Angels says it was not. Both are right.
- **The one thing Sassy can always do:** make him eat. It is the series' smallest recurring beat and the one that keeps the saviour a boy.
- **Their look:** nothing exists and nothing is requested here. Direction only, for the briefs when Ziggy wants them. Angels: old, weathered, grey in the stubble, the same desert suit in an older cut, no cloak, no marks, no piercings, a thumper-maker's hands, the iron on his back. Sassy: older than Lisan, not old; strong hands; a head wrap against the dust; the camp's one bright cloth; no marks; never confusable with the fifty panels' women. Neither is ever confusable with Lisan or the Keepers.
- **Their voices:** neither is the whisper (the whisper is Lisan early and Jet late, DECISIONS item 10). If either is in the song, it is at their own panels (§5).
