## 1. What the record holds (canon and its labels)

Three Keepers stand between the people and the pump and fill the cups: half for a child, a third for a man, a swallow for the old. They keep the knowing (how the pump works: the valve that seizes every season, the choked line behind it, the wind-scoop above, closed for forty years).
