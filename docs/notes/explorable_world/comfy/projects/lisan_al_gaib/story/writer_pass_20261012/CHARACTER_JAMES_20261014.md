## 2. The man, as seen at full size (reviewer claude; a description, not a grade)

Tight dark curls; a wide open smile with natural teeth; gold hoop earrings in both ears; two gold chains; small tattoos on the cheek under the eye and script on the neck; a black leather overcoat worn open over a bare chest carrying a large compass-like mandala; lettering and figures on the stomach, the left forearm (viewer-right) and the back of the left hand, the right arm sleeved to the hip; black trousers, a wide belt, an ornately tooled holster on his left hip (viewer-right) with a revolver in it, to be removed; standing square to camera in a hangar with a ramp and a lit ship's mouth behind him, steam on a wet floor. **What he does not carry:** the AL, the chin lettering, the throat worms and the eye-disc, the blue eyes, the breather. He is unmistakably not Lisan, and that is the point of him.

## 3. What a brother does to the continuity **[proposed]**

- **The sign skipped him.** Story 01 makes Lisan's marks "the dead sign," his mother's, the sign the Keepers name and the line parts for. James has none. The simplest continuity, and the richest: two sons of the same mother, and the sign came down to one of them. James is the brother who was spared it, or passed over by it, and which of those he believes is his whole character.
- **The smile is his.** Lisan is the one who holds still. James is the one who grins in a hangar with a ship behind him: the brother who left, or the brother who trades, who moves between settlements and the city under the cliff and the raiders' market (Seed 02's bazaar sells knowing by the cup; Seed 03's rig is built from a wreck graveyard; Episode 5's convoy has a standoff). The picture's setting, a hangar and a ship, says he goes where the ships are. **[proposed; the setting is Hon Dolo's hangar in the source and is not Lisan canon; what it suggests is a brother with reach.]**
- **Angels and Sassy raised both, or one.** The Angels and Sassy note proposes, not yet with Ziggy's word, that they raised Lisan after the mother died. Either they raised James too and he left (the son who would not stay for the water line), or James was already gone when she died and comes back when the lamps are seen from far off. The second gives Story 01's tower a second reader: the light that blinks back from the dunes is answered later by the brother who saw the lamps from the ships. **[proposed; two options for Ziggy, not a decision.]**
- **The Panhandlers and the Fall.** James does the shadow work or he does not; if he does, he is a Panhandler of the mother's own line without the sign, which tests whether the sign runs in the blood or lands where it chooses (unmarked Panhandlers already exist: Sassy, the child, the thousandth); if he does not, he is Angels' ally in doubt with a younger face. In the Fall branch (Episode 4's voice, Episode 8's devil with Lisan's eyes), the brother is the one who would see it first, because he knew the boy before the name. **[proposed]**
- **Time.** In the forty-year loop, the brother is the one who can be at both ends without a mirror: the same man at two ages, which the time well's shape (Seed 09, TIME_AND_THE_FALL) already asks for in the people at the fire. A grown James at the tower's reply is the loop's cleanest proof. **[proposed; and it depends on the age Ziggy gives him.]**
- **What stays separate:** the gun. The derivative removes it, and the writer keeps it removed in every scene: James does not carry a weapon in the Lisan world unless Ziggy says so; the holster stays as a thing he wears empty, which is a story in itself.
