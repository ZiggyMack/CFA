# James stencil tolerance spec: PRELIMINARY, FOR REVIEW (not approved)
Source: the clean v004 reference, 957897bd45c4__HIP-COAT-01_JAMES-CLEAN_v004.png (sha256 35bc7b17…7ee0). It has no gun and no holster.
Note: the hero cockpit shot (JAMES-V2-002 v002) was NOT used as the source, because his hair drifted straight in it. The stencils come from the locked v002 reference.

Each feature has one master (M00) plus five variants (V01–V05). The variants show the expected drift: a shift of ±4%, a scale of 95–105%, and a rotation of ±3–5°. The tolerances below are measured relative to the size of each feature.

| Feature | Pass | Soft fail (keep, grade 3) | Reject |
|---|---|---|---|
| Face | Shift ≤5%, scale 93–107%, rotation ≤5°; eyes, nose, smile and beard line overlay the master | 5–10% shift or 5–10° rotation; one feature off | >10% off, a different person, or the smile or beard missing |
| Hair | Curly volume within ±10% of the master outline; dark, tight curls | Volume ±10–20%, or looser curls | Straight, bald or short hair (until Ziggy decides on the future head shave) |
| Silhouette | Build within ±7% of the shoulder and torso width; the long open coat is present | ±7–15% (bulkier or slimmer) | Clearly heavier or slimmer build (proportion drift), or no long coat |
| Tattoos | Astrolabe is circular, centred on the sternum, size ±10%; neck, face, abdomen and forearm zones are present | One minor zone missing, or the astrolabe off-centre by up to 10% | Astrolabe missing or not circular, tattoos on the wrong body side, or chest ink replaced |

Linework detail inside the tattoos is soft: overall shape and placement are judged, not each line.
v002 set changes: the tattoo master and the zone stencils are now clean redrawn flash: crisp black lines with no speckle. The redraws follow the general shapes of the original tattoos; they are not exact traces, so judge placement and overall form. The hair stencil follows a curved hairline and excludes the brows and eyes. The silhouette comes from v004, so there is no holster, and its holes are filled. The face stencil has its background removed and small speckle cleared.
