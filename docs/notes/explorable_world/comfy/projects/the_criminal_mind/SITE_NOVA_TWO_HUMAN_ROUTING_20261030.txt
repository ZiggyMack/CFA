ZIGGY'S CLARIFICATION

Baldadoon's projects are his world. Default human reviews, project decisions and unassigned human work for The Criminal Mind go to Casa Baldadoon. Hon Dolo and Lisan Al Gaib are Ziggy's world and keep Ziggy as their default controller. Two humans may contribute to one project: Ziggy may receive a specific assistance, operational, issue-resolution or project-related task when his involvement is needed. Do not exclude him merely because the underlying project belongs to Baldadoon, and do not send every Baldadoon decision to both humans.

ROUTING MODEL

Keep project owner/default controller, task assignee, decision authority/scope and actual reviewer as distinct concepts. A task defaults to its project's controller unless explicitly assigned elsewhere. An explicit Ziggy assignment appears as actionable work in Ziggy's Corner, retains its Baldadoon-project identity, and can remain visible in Casa as delegated work. It is one linked task, not two independently editable copies. Link back to its originating project and Casa context.

Assigning Ziggy a bounded task does not transfer the project to him or make every future task require both humans. A technical help task can be resolved by its assignee; a final creative verdict remains with Baldadoon unless its decision authority is explicitly delegated. Preserve the actual actor's name and the allowed scope when a delegated human does make a decision. No blanket two-person approval gate is requested.

Unassigned project work follows the default controller. Genuine platform or shared operational work can be explicitly assigned to Ziggy under the existing task conventions, rather than manufacturing a Baldadoon creative decision. No blanket rule that all issues must route to Ziggy was requested. If a task cannot proceed without the other human, make the requested action and dependency visible, with the original owner preserved; completing the help task returns the dependent project work to its owning flow.

USER EXPERIENCE AND VERIFICATION

Casa shows Baldadoon's actionable queue and the status of work delegated to Ziggy. Ziggy's Corner shows tasks explicitly assigned to Ziggy, including help on a Baldadoon project, alongside his normal Hon/Lisan work. Show project, owner and assignee clearly enough to explain why each task is present. Avoid duplicate counts or implying that a delegated task is simultaneously awaiting two verdicts.
