# The Criminal Mind: the writer's questions for the author (prepared 2026-11-01 by Claude, lead writer; relayed by Ziggy)

How to answer: reply in any order, by letter; a word or a sentence each is enough; "writer's default" is what the writer will do if a question stays open. Line numbers are the screenplay's lines in the intake text. Nothing here changes the script; where a question touches the plot, the writer does not repair it, the author decides. The three that shape the most work right now are B, K and O.

## Ask first

**B. Era and the phone.** The phone slammed shut (818) and the cell phone of line 64 are the only form-factor details the script gives. The flip-phone years with a flip phone, or present day with a smartphone and an anonymous incoming call?
Options: flip-phone years, flip phone / present day, smartphone. Writer's default: period-neutral with the flip phone (G7); the set round keeps every cabin and desk free of modern screens either way. Why it matters: Shapes every phone insert, the car's dash, the office screens and the television; the blocked-number displays (248, 295) and the split screen (1690) pull two ways. (lines 64, 248, 295, 818, 1534, 1690)

**K. The agency.** State Police (525, 1809), a PD garage (325), a precinct (516), barracks and a sled (394), a federal case over state lines (379). One agency name for the signage, badges and cars?
Options: Massachusetts State Police throughout / a city police department / another. Writer's default: Massachusetts State Police throughout, since the screenplay names it twice and the others are colloquial. Why it matters: Signage, badges, cars and the precinct's exterior; the set round shoots everything unlettered meanwhile. (lines 325, 379, 394, 516, 525, 1809)

**O. Screen length (for Ziggy and Baldadoon together).** The whole screenplay as one film (the writer pass, Part 9, estimates its length), or a contained excerpt first? This is the site card's open question, choose the adaptation scope.
