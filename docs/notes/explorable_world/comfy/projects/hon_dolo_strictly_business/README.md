## Central irony

**The greedy smuggler who insists everything is strictly business sacrifices himself for love.** Ziggy's October 4 clarification makes this contradiction the center of the video. Keep his greed, vulgarity, charm and mercenary instincts convincing throughout; love makes his actions contradict his own terms. The ending should sharpen the irony of the opening boasts.

The ship's return introduces the contradiction. Leia, his request that Chewie protect her, the farewell and carbonite bring it to its most personal expression. Love costs the man who demands payment his own freedom. This does not require him to knowingly choose carbonite or imply that he dies. His scoundrel attitude can survive the sacrifice: the images expose the cost while he still answers with his familiar confidence.

Chewie can be the second storyteller. At the decks he creates momentum; in the cockpit he sees the contradiction in Hon's excuses; at the farewell he becomes the person trusted to keep Leia safe. His MC/DJ role can connect these scenes through recurring poses, controls and reactions.

Love, self-sacrifice and the irony of his greedy identity are confirmed direction. The specific shots, deeper abstract ideas, crossover allusions and balance of comedy and tenderness remain open for Ziggy's direction.

3. Select a shot card and accepted character/setting references. Assign design, appearance, driving performance and audio roles separately. Replace the card's null paths and times only with actual inspected assets.
4. Map that card to one verified saved or official template. For lip-sync, provide the matching recorded vocal excerpt; for an insert, use the visual brief and retain its intended edit time. 6. Assemble selected takes against the original song master. Treat generated sound as a separate optional track. Regeneration order follows the chosen provider contract; retain unmodified originals for any later provider finishing step.
