## Reusable procedure

Keep originals, assign one explicit role to each chosen reference, preserve its hash/source path, and compare against the actual accepted appearance before generating. This applies Panhandlers' [reference and crop lesson](../../../../../../../../Pan_Handlers/Help/content/cfa_learning.py) and CFA's [reference-check recipe](../../../workflows/README.md#choose-by-problem). Source QA claims such as IP-Adapter will fix it are untested for these assets.

When Ziggy marks the best files, update the reference map with those exact filenames and roles. A wardrobe choice should not also replace the face, grill or tattoo map. Preserve superseded ideas and rejection reasons in the journey record.
