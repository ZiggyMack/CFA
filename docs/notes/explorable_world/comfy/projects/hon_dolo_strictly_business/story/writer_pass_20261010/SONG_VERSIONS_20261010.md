## 3. Recommendation

**Do not pick one. Pick an order, and give each flavor a job.** The song is about saying the same thing until it means its opposite; four versions of the same words is that device at a larger scale, which is the honest creative reason this is not self-indulgence. It becomes self-indulgence only if the versions are the same video in four coats of paint. So each version gets a verb and a world:

| Version | Verb | World and what changes | What it shares |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1, the original | **says it** | The Prime cut: the full story spine as written, Prime Dolo, the clearest words for the clearest telling. Finish this first. | Everything: it is the master. |
| 2, the radio cut | **sells it** | The club world: the cantina, Chewie on the decks, the crowd, the swag of the inspirational video. Mostly performance frames (A and C), the story carried by inserts. This is also the version the characters themselves would hear playing in the cantina, which makes it the natural diegetic track for the frame story's counter scene. | The cards, the locks, the cut logic; new set dressing and a different cut rhythm. |
| 3, the rage cut | **screams it** | Multiverse Dolo: the armor-swap lane, a different universe's Dolo, the action heavy (Kessel run, the turn, the battle light). | The spine; a scoped alternate look already permitted by rule. |
| 4, the metal cut | **pays it** | The dark one: the dinner, the farewell, the slab, the quarters epilogue; the sections where the price comes due carry the weight. | The spine; frames B and D dominate. |

Two rules that keep this cheap instead of ruinous. First, **each version differs from Prime in one dimension** (world, set dressing, cut rhythm), never everything; the scene cards are written to frames and motifs, not seconds, so they transfer. Second, **what cannot transfer is the audio work**: every recording needs its own listening pass for timing and its own lip-sync inserts. That is the real cost of a version, and it is the reason to finish one before starting two.

**The lyrics.** If the words are identical across versions, one lyric master serves all four. If a version's delivery already changes a line (the metal cut may), that version needs its own lyric master file and its own section map, and the spine marks the sections that diverge. A changed word is a changed lock.

**The stretch idea, flagged as a stretch.** One long piece whose soundtrack changes weather with the story: the cab prologue silent; the counter and club in the radio cut, diegetic; the ship in the original; the turn and the battle in the rage cut; the dinner and the slab in the metal cut; the outro back in the original. Same words throughout; the audience hears the song turn into its own opposite. It is the mind-blowing version Ziggy is reaching for, and it is also the hardest: tempos differ (the original is slower), so section joins need a producer's bridges, and every section is cut to its own recording. I would not plan it until Prime is accepted and at least one second flavor has its timing pass. Then it is an edit, not a new production.

**What to decide now:** nothing except the order. Version 1 first. Which recordings on disk correspond to which flavor. Whether versions 2 through 4 are destinations (arena, art channel) or layers of one piece. All three can wait until there is one accepted take.
