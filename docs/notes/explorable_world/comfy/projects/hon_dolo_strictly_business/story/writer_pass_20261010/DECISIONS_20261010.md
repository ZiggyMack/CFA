## 6. New input, same evening: more than one version of the song (undecided)

Ziggy, with a full disclaimer that he has not decided: he has at least two recordings of the song he cannot choose between, possibly a third and a fourth. One idea is to use one for the arena he is competing in and another for his art channel, in a different flavor; another is that the versions could be the Multiverse Dolo device, so the same lyrics get more than one shot at telling a larger story, with the lyrics possibly varying slightly between versions.

Record only: two recordings already exist in CFA's music intake (the October 4 and October 7 masters, with different lengths and different audio, same written words). Any further versions are not yet on disk.

My recommendation, for Ziggy to accept or reject, not a decision:

1. **Finish one first.** The spine, the scene cards and every lock are version-independent as long as the words are the same; what changes per version is timing, lip-sync audio and flavor. The acceptance bottleneck is already the whole project's bottleneck. One version accepted end to end teaches us more than two half-done; call it the Prime cut.
2. **Then the versions are cheap, not expensive.** Because the cards are written to frames and motifs, a second version can reuse the same cards with a different look (the Multiverse armor-swap lane already exists as a scoped collection), a different set dressing or a different cut rhythm, and only the lip-sync inserts have to be remade. That is exactly what the Multiverse device is for: same man, same words, different world, and the audience sees a larger story across versions.
3. **The lyrics should not drift between versions unless the story needs it.** A changed word is a changed lock (the lyric master is the source for every card). If Ziggy wants variants, each variant needs its own lyric master file and its own section map, and the spine notes which sections differ.
4. **One version per channel is a distribution decision, not a story decision.** The story can be the same; the flavor sells the channel.

Nothing in this section changes a file or a plan. It is here so the next session knows the question exists.
