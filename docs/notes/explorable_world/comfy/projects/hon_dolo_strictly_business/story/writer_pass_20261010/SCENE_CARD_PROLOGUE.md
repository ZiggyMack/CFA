## Story beat

Why the video is about to happen. A woman with information gets into Dolo's cab. This card's proposal for the exchange: she has no fare, so she pays with the only currency she has, the tip from the confirmed weave (DECISIONS section 2): a princess needs a ride off this rock and has money. He takes the tip as the fare, drops the informant where she asks, walks into the cantina, sits at the counter, and the first chorus starts on the customer the tip was about. The song is Dolo's pitch to one specific person, and the prologue is how he knew to make it. The informant's name, backstory and exact information remain open in the record; "no fare" is a story proposal, not a biography.

What this does for the piece: it puts the irony in place before a note plays (a man who charges for everything has just been paid in information), it gives the informant a job without inventing a biography, and it keeps her out of the song's ending, so the farewell stays between him and Leia. Whether she returns after the song, as the witness at the thaw, is open (DECISIONS section 5).

Constraints carried from the record: the informant is not Leia and has no name, backstory or relationship assigned (cast intake, Ziggy's own words: "a unknown generic informant for now"); her pictured look (short silver hair with pink ends, cream cropped jacket, mechanical forearm, wet neon ledge) is external inspiration only and no production adaptation is accepted; Dolo's face, mouth (external-off, four gold caps, two plain lip hoops), cheek lettering, neck blaster, chest ship and wrist accessories are the current references exactly as the cab handoff lists them; the wrist side is set explicitly for this scene and is not canon; one coherent outfit must be chosen before cabin frames (this card proposes the opening outfit; no source records that choice for the cab); the first proofs are silent; dialogue is a separately prepared step; the r4 iced look is not imported. Whether the informant returns in the epilogue is still open (DECISIONS section 5).

## Screen geography

The landing is at screen-right; the cab arrives from screen-left and stops alongside with its passenger door and step facing the landing; she boards moving screen-left into the cabin. The cab leaves by lifting, turning its nose away from camera and sliding into the traffic in depth, so it never crosses the landing it came alongside; the same at her stop. Interior: a side-by-side cabin seen from the front through the windscreen, Dolo at the controls screen-left, the informant in the passenger seat screen-right; her eyeline to him is screen-left, his glance to her is screen-right. The cantina, as the choruses card lays it out: the entrance on the front wall, the booth on the back wall, the counter along the right-hand wall. The cantina walk (P-7) is shot from the booth's side looking toward the entrance, the reverse of S01-2's view, so the door is in frame and he walks toward camera to the counter at screen-right.

The wrist chain and dark band go on the character-right wrist for this scene, matching the opening prototype's provisional blocking choice, so the frame story and the song agree without either claiming canon.
