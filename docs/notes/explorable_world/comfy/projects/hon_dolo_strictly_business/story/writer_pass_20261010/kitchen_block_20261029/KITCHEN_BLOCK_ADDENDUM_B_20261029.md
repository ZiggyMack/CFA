**Acknowledged, in one paragraph.** A block has four maturities and they never trade places. Its **source foundation** is the maturity of the one explicitly selected complete scene image that conditions it, or of each selected required input by role when the mode binds several; it is read from the record's stage, scope and hash, never inferred. Its **authoring maturity** is the card's own state (proposed detailed design today for every Hon shot) and the site's storyboard stage, which the indicator never writes. Its **technical readiness** is whether the preparation step is done, the bindings observed and the checks current (11.9; section 8). Its **result** is the actual video and Ziggy's verdict on it (sections 9 and 10). A mature frame saves preparation work; it does not establish the new action, the camera move, the musical timing, the lip sync or the result, each of which stays pending until its own evidence exists. Under the October 12 method the team may still carry qualifying scene work through Detailed design without asking Ziggy to approve a settled reference again; this is not a new approval gate, and a changed input flags dependent checks for review rather than rewriting any stage, review or result. The seventy authored shots have no documented prepared starting frame today, so none is promoted by the mature components it links.


## Part 1. The foundation line in 11.2

One line, added after the input rows of every block from here on, in this form:

`Foundation: <state>. Selected starting image: <asset id, repoPath, sha256, source stage with its scope and date, review status>, or none. Required inputs by role: <role> <asset id>: <stage as reviewed, or "stage not recorded", or "stage: none"; the recorded selection, if any>; ... Mode: <first_frame | ingredients | edit | extend | from_scratch | image_free>, the mode the saved binding records where a binding exists, otherwise the mode the block expects the route to bind, marked expected. Planned preparation: <STILL PREP from prompt 7, from scratch | STILL PREP from the selected image | not required, because ... | none of its own, inherits <shot>'s>.`

**The states, in the site's words** (root rules, items 1 to 3; the labels are reused, not renamed):

| State | When a block carries it | What is written beside it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Detailed foundation | an explicitly selected complete scene starting image whose recorded stage is Detailed design, Verification or Production | the exact source stage ("source Production", "source Verification", "source Detailed design"), its review scope and date, its hash |
| Preliminary foundation | an explicitly selected scene starting image whose recorded stage is Preliminary | the same facts |
| Maturity not recorded | an explicitly selected scene image with no recorded stage | the selection evidence (who, when, where recorded) and "stage not recorded"; never written as Preliminary |
| No starting frame selected | no selected scene image, whatever components or panels the block links | the required inputs by role with their own maturity, so the components stay visible as components |
| From scratch | the site's selection record holds a documented exact-shot binding for the block whose recorded mode is from_scratch, with its evidence (who, when, where recorded) and no required media input; the writer's planned preparation is intent and is not that record | the prompt's location (7, or a set-only 7s); required inputs by role: none, because the site reads a from-scratch binding that carries any required media input as unresolved input, not From scratch; a component the prompt describes rather than attaches keeps its maturity on its own 11.2 input row (rule 4) and is not listed in the line |
| Not required | a route whose binding records the mode image_free, documented in the same record and in 11.12 | why no image is needed |

**Which of the two no-image states a block reads.** The site reads From scratch and Not required from one record only: a documented exact-shot selection in `visual_library/storyboard_input_bindings.json` (format cfa-storyboard-input-bindings-v1; its note, in its words: only documented exact-shot selections belong here; linked art, legacy shot-plan crosswalks and inspiration are not selections; no prepared starting frame is documented for the current 70 Hon authored shots), saved by the operator at dispatch when a from-scratch prompt is the route chosen or when the route needs no image. The writer saves nothing there and never writes either state ahead of it. A from-scratch intent in the block's "Planned preparation" slot is the writer's plan, not a binding, and leaves the state at No starting frame selected. Today that record holds no bindings, so every block in Part 3 reads No starting frame selected with its from-scratch intent written beside it.

**Vocabulary for a component's maturity on its own row.** "production", "verification", "detailed_design" or "preliminary" as the canonical review file records the storyboard stage, with its scope (detail, identity, scene, outfit, complete_look) and date; "stage not recorded" where the file holds a review of the asset without a stage; "stage: none" where the file holds no entry for the asset at all. The catalog's intake label and the BOM's scoped selection stay on the row as the first two parts of the status triplet; the stage is read from the third part only.

**Rules the line enforces.**

- Only an explicitly selected complete scene starting image gives a foundation. A panel in 11.3, a graded frame marked inspiration, a named identity reference and a scoped component (mouth, cheek, wrist, chest, neck) never do, however mature; their maturity is written on their own row and stops there (rule 4). The writer never picks an image to fill the line (rule 1) and never infers a selection from a filename or a folder.
- In `ingredients`, `edit` and `extend` modes the line reports every selected required input in its own role, a `source_video` included, and does not take the highest stage across them; a mixed set reads as mixed (rule 5).
- A missing file, a hash that does not match the record, or two selection records that disagree is written as "unresolved input" on that row; the foundation state is shown with that flag beside whatever can still be read, never as a clean grade, and 11.9 names the dependent checks and packs that are stale until it is resolved (rule 6).
- A selected input's known differences, defects and scoped exceptions, whether it is the starting image or a scoped component, travel with it onto its row (mouth B's cropped nose jewelry, recorded in the BOM's known differences; wrist F's unseen hand and wrist side, recorded in the review file's scope note of 2026-10-14); the foundation state does not launder them. An inspiration frame is not selected, so its defects stay on its inspiration row and travel nowhere by themselves (the cockpit candidate's grill and neck: needs fixes, preliminary, 2026-10-09, no BOM selection); they would be inherited only if a route later bound that frame as a selected input, which no block does today, and S02-6's 11.9 names that as a condition, not a selection.
- The site's field "start · Prepared starting frame" (one of the 28 fields of the blank template published today, owned by the operator at dispatch) is filled from the record, not from the writer; the writer's 11.2 is where the selection evidence it reads must already stand, and where "none" is written plainly when there is none.


## Part 2. The consequence lines in 11.9 and 11.12

**11.9 gains one line.** `Foundation consequence:` what the state means for this block: what must be prepared before the mode can run; what the result inherits from the selected image's recorded defects and exceptions, named so the review scores inheritance as inheritance; and which dependent checks and packs go stale if the selected image's hash changes (the clip pack, the Flow brief, the Grok request, the Comfy intake, any evidence recorded against the old hash).

**11.12 gains one line.** `Foundation decisions:` who selects a starting image (Ziggy selects or accepts; the operator binds it by hash at dispatch and copies its recorded stage and scope from the canonical review file, writing neither; the writer records the selection in 11.2 and proposes none); and the input-change rule: a new hash for the same creative text is a **re-binding of the same take** with its dependent checks and packs flagged for review, not a creative revision, and never a rewrite of old stages, results, reviews or accepted decisions. This sits beside Addendum A's 2.5, which stays as written: a changed creative section (2 to 6) is a new take; a changed input is a new binding. The two can coincide, and then both happen and both are written.

**What does not change.** The storyboard stage and the human reviews (320 saved decisions, preserved by the site index's publication of today); the one-clip rule for scope B; the status triplet in 11.2; the cue and arc lines of Addendum A; the names rule; timing null until alignment.
