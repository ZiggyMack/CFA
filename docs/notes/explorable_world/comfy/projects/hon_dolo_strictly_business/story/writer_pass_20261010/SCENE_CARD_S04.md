## Story beat

She paid. She boarded. Now she looks at what she paid for, and he cannot stand it. The verse opens on her name sung twice and ends on the beat drop, and in between he does the thing the whole song is about: he defends the junky ship like a home, promises to protect her, and demands his fee in the same breath. The softened face is held one beat too long before the swagger comes back; that beat is the whole song. Chewie closes the section with one fader move, and the lights answering him is the hand-off to the ignition.

Constraints carried from the record: Leia's first scene is here; the S03 glance's reverse on her across the counter, one beat, belongs to the choruses card and precedes this section if it is used; mouth external-off; no two-character frames unless a card says so (every shot below is a single); the cockpit geography is S07's (Chewie screen-left of Dolo, the display screen-right); the chest ship is upright twin-spire per CFA, not SB-17's horizontal one; no Vader; timing null.

**One story question for Ziggy, not resolved here.** The October 9 cast review records "Leia is Hon Dolo's wife" (the ivory-coat portrait as her cover). The song's frame has her as the princess with money, the customer across the counter, and the pass writes her that way. If she is his wife inside the story, the counter and the fee read as a couple's running bit, which is funnier and changes nothing structurally, but S04-2's eyebrow becomes a wife's, not a stranger's. The card is written for the customer reading; the wife reading is one line of direction per shot and can be switched on by a decision.

## Cut logic

1 → 2 on her stopping to her looking. 2 → 3 on her eyeline (screen-left, to the cockpit) to him turning back to meet it (over the shoulder, screen-right-rear): the two looks meet across the cut. 3 → 4 on his hand on the console to his hand on the shirt. 4 → 5 on the shirt falling to the hands rising. 5 → 6 on the status light to his face, the first still frame after three moving hands. 6 → 7 on the swagger's return to Chewie's hand, the fastest cut in the section. 7 → S05 on the pulse to the straps.

Section rhythm: she looks (1–2), he answers (3–6), the crew answers him (7). Four of the seven shots are hands or an insert; only S04-2 and S04-6 are faces held long.
