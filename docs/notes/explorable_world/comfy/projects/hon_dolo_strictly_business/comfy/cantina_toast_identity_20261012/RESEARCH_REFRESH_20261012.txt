GAP3 - IDENTITY-SPECIFIC COMPARISON
FaceFusion 3.9.1 uses face alignment/swap/paste-back on existing frames, reference selection/tracking, region/occlusion masks and separate LivePortrait target-expression restoration with upper/lower-face controls. ReActor 0.7.1 offers Comfy image-batch swaps with optional masks/restoration. Both are plausible identity-only comparisons. Neither guarantees identical blinks or semantic protection for tattoo lettering, individual jewelry or grills. Tracking does not establish flicker-free video. Retain explicit source-protection compositing.
