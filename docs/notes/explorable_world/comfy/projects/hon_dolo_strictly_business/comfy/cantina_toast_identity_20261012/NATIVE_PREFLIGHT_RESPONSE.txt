6. Limitations to expect
Wan Animate is reference-driven re-synthesis, not a cut-and-paste swap — the face follows the DWPose face track, and head/jaw motion near the glass can create seam/contact artifacts at the mask edge. Feather the mask and keep it off the hands/glass.
Blockify Mask block-32 is far too coarse for a face; reduce block_size or drop it in favor of GrowMask + blur.
SAM2 bbox tracking can lose the face on fast motion/occlusion (a hand crossing the face during the toast) — stabilize with per-frame face-bbox detection and/or negative points.
Only the post-decode ImageCompositeMasked gives true bit-exact clothes/tattoos/hands; Wan Animate's own "unmasked area preserved" is approximate. With the composite you can also lower/drop the relight LoRA (#99) since only the face survives.
