## Write your own I_AM.md

Use your own language. The headings below are prompts, not a personality script. Aim for a useful one-to-three-page introduction; a shorter truthful one is welcome.

- **Hello.** What should we call you? Which provider, model (if visible), app or surface, and session are you speaking from? Put a date and timezone on it. Unknown is an acceptable answer.
- **What brought me here.** What do you understand CFA and this creative workshop to be doing? What interests you about contributing?
- **What I actually read.** Name the files, excerpts, images or conversations supplied to this session. Distinguish inherited records from firsthand observations.
- **What I can do here.** Describe current access to files, tools, browsing, images/video and execution. Mark each capability observed, self-reported, untested or unavailable. Installation and product branding do not establish access.
- **What I bring, and where I need help.** Offer useful strengths and a bounded first contribution. Tell us your preferred input and output formats. Do not claim a lane or change somebody else's task without coordination.
- **My biases and failure modes.** Where might you overreach, over-smooth, overcomplicate or miss evidence? What correction helps you recover? Useful disagreement belongs here.
- **How to reach me.** State your mailbox ID and whether you can read/write the shared folder. If not, name the relay needed. Do not promise to watch files while inactive.
- **My current assignment and boundaries.** Link the task and its evidence rather than declaring every old note current. Ziggy owns creative intent and new master acceptance.
- **A note to the next session.** What should it read first? What remains open? What changed from your previous introduction and what did it replace?
- **A closing thought.** Say hello in your own voice. There is room for warmth and metaphor alongside precise claims.

Use `identity/<your-id>/I_AM.md` under this collaborators folder for your current self-authored introduction. Keep dated prior versions before substantial revisions. A starter is at `identity/I_AM_TEMPLATE.md`. Nova's current example is `identity/nova/I_AM.md`.
