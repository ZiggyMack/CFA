CFA REFERENCE SELECTION AND LIKENESS RATINGS

A 1-5 likeness rating describes how closely the pictured role matches its
gold standard. Unscored files remain Unrated. The score is separate from a
favorite star, design stage, Prime/Multiverse lane, approval and scope. A 5/5
wrist reference cannot govern a face. Keep known differences in the review note.

An explicitly named scene reference wins for that scene only. A deliberately
chosen 3/5 image may be useful for that shot; it does not replace the character's
canonical face, mouth or other locked components. For other scenes, or a
dealer's-choice request, begin with the canonical scoped gold-standard references
and the highest-rated suitable approved alternatives. Do not reuse a lower-rated
scene variation by habit. Do not invent scores for existing files.

Production means the image or task is approved for its recorded role. A scene may
need a background or prop fix while its independently locked character components
remain usable. Approval of a mouth macro, wardrobe or tattoo does not approve the
whole character; image stage does not change a separate component lock. Prime can
contain accepted close variations without replacing the canonical identity.

Working references are the character's approved bill of materials: current cover,
scoped components and permitted alternatives, with scenes/motion kept separate.
Removal marks hide files from ordinary browsing and handoff selection; history,
review records and original bytes are retained. No physical deletion is implied.

Carry exact asset IDs, SHA-256 hashes, role, rating, known differences and scene
intent with every file drop. A selection list contains metadata, not media; attach
the actual files when delivering it. Browser choices need Save review file or an
export to become a durable host handoff.
Whole-scene readiness is a separate manual review. B or better is the production target, with every required ingredient reviewed ready, no critical blockers, and an explicitly verified shoot plan (direction, camera and lighting, reviewer and review time). A missing character, incomplete hands or another critical defect cannot be averaged away. Grade and shoot-plan review never change character identity, component locks, design stage or final generated-result acceptance. Ungraded, blocked and unbound plans remain available as planning or repair handoffs.

Shoot-plan verification guide: docs/notes/explorable_world/comfy/projects/collaborators/work_briefs_20261010/SHOOT_PLAN_VERIFICATION.txt

HON PIERCING ASYMMETRY - OCTOBER 10, 2026, OPTIONAL FINAL STYLING NOTE
The paired nose/lip piercing standard identifies the intended features, not a mandatory mirrored placement. Subtle natural differences in height, angle or spacing are acceptable and are not, alone, a scoring defect. Symmetric references remain valid. Consider this only within a future already-requested final styling pass; do not regenerate existing files, rerate them, reopen approvals or create repair tasks solely to add imperfections. Preserve ring type, the selected scene's piercing state and continuity. The selected iced r4/quarters look intentionally has no lip rings. This note changes no user decisions or approvals and requests no generation.

SCENE CRAFT AND CHARACTER LIKENESS - OCTOBER 11, 2026
Keep two independent 1-5 image assessments. likenessRating judges the character
or scoped component against its canonical gold standard. sceneQualityRating
(Scene craft) judges framing, camera, lighting, staging and atmosphere. A 2/5
likeness and 5/5 scene can coexist: preserve the shot's strengths while documenting
a targeted character correction. These are examples, not assigned user scores.
Never average these scores, substitute scene craft for character fidelity, or
use it to rank default character references. Default selection still uses the
highest suitable approved likenessRating and canonical scoped references.
Favorites, Keep / Needs fixes / Remove, creative lane and design stage remain
independent. Neither score approves production or changes a component lock.
Whole-scene readiness retains its separate B-or-better gate, required ingredients
and shoot-plan verification. Preserve both scores and the correction/preservation
note in provider handoffs. Unrated is unknown, not a five. No existing images are
rerated, regenerated, approved or discarded by adding this field.

CREATIVE REVIEW REQUESTS - OCTOBER 11, 2026
The viewer's Creative review buttons select specific named reviewers. This is
separate from Plan a shot and its generation-provider destinations. A story-use
request asks the selected reviewer to consider an image for the music video OR
stories between videos; it does not require a current song placement. Include
known character differences, scene strengths, exact reference ID/hash and notes.
Keep the department/purpose and actual recipients distinct: a story-review purpose
never means broadcast to every writer or collaborator.

Selecting a reviewer saves items[].creativeReview in the review file/browser and
can export cfa-creative-review-requests.json. Those records are queued, not sent.
Nova relays an exported/saved request through the named recipient's verified
route, with separate delivery/read receipts. No automatic mailbox watcher,
notification, app wake, generation or acceptance is claimed. Existing shot plans
and provider destinations are preserved; they are not migrated into review
requests or duplicated. A creative review of a multiverse idea does not turn it
into the default Prime identity. Removal-marked images stay in backup records
and are excluded from outbound review-request exports.
