ONE-PAGE EXPERT RETURN
1. Lighting intention: preserve blue night ambience; identify existing practical
   lamps and propose their motivated warm contribution, position/direction and
   warm-cool relationship. Separate observed cues from assumptions.
2. Local behavior: falloff, occlusion, shadows, material response and reflections
   consistent with geometry. Preserve plausible skin and silver-jacket colors;
   avoid a global yellow wash or invented lights that alter the scene.
3. Camera design: framing, movement, coverage and subject emphasis, tied to the
   storyboard intention and continuity. Identify the proposed change and whether
   it needs regeneration; it must not silently alter the fixed-camera lighting
   experiment. Unknown lens or camera settings remain unknown.
4. One concise prompt for the verified target edit mode, with explicit invariants
   and tool limits. Reference the existing proposed C prompt-v2 adapter; consolidate
   suggestions into that single proposal rather than silently creating more runs.
5. Evaluation checklist: local warm source behavior; retained blue mood; coherent
   shadows/reflections; preserved identity/outfit/action/timing/contact; no new
   cuts/objects/audio; separate inherited defects and resolution observations.
6. Bounded example analysis: begin with the three previously supplied CFA examples
   (cab-exit woman; character pulling a sword from stone; futuristic bike in sand).
   PILOT_PACKAGE.txt records their exact links/intakes and attributed archived-still
   evidence. Full motion is unverified; live Civitai access attempts failed. Do not
   guess internet matches. If actual Civitai visual/video/still access is available,
   examine up to these three supplied examples and
   cite their pages. Separate observed lighting/camera from creator-documented
   model/version/settings and technique hypotheses. Incomplete recipes can still
   provide useful visual targets; still-image recipes do not establish transfer
   to Flow video editing. No live creator-workflow research was added by this draft.
   If access is insufficient, record it; Ziggy can curate additional exact links
   after the existing examples are used. Proposed division: Ziggy as curator,
   specialist as analyst and CFA as operator, with no role assigned by this draft.
