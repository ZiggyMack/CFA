CREATIVE ROLES ROTATE THROUGH REAL WORK
Casting: candidates and scoped identity. Writing: meaningful story/scene beats.
Direction: intention, action, blocking, performance and eyelines.
Cinematography: framing, angle, movement, lens intent and lighting.
Set design: geography, atmosphere and story-bearing backgrounds.
Props: objects, counts, materials and hand contact. Costume: select early,
preserve approved construction. VFX: effects/finishing with preservation goals.
Continuity: identity, mouth, tattoos, jewelry, hands, props and temporal joins.
One collaborator can cover several roles; tool count does not prove skill.
A chat-only director can return a strong plan without a render button.

THREE REVIEWS THAT MUST STAY SEPARATE
1. Asset likeness: 1-5 stars compare the pictured role to its scoped gold
   standard, not favoritism, garment appeal or provider skill. A 5/5 wrist
   reference is not face authority. Unrated remains unrated.
2. Scene readiness: B or better is the production-handoff target, with every
   required ingredient reviewed Ready, no critical blocker, and an actual
   verified direction/camera/lighting plan carrying reviewer and time.
   Ready characters alone do not make a ready scene.
3. Collaborator role score: grade that author's actual named contribution.
   Track quality, consistency, measured time, rework, observed cost and tool
   limits separately. Asset stars/scene grades are not AI grades.

Freeze the first independent attempt including failures. Assisted revisions
remain separate, naming who supplied text, used tools or executed commands.
Unknown stays unknown; one image is not a success rate or universal winner.
I_AM preferences and capability claims are not earned grades.

REFERENCE SELECTION AND HANDOFF
Working references are the approved bill of materials: cover, scoped components
and permitted alternatives, with scenes/motion separate. Production approves
the recorded role; a liked outfit, mouth macro or tattoo sheet does not approve
every feature or replace whole-character authority.

A named scene exception governs that scene only. Otherwise use canonical scoped
gold standards and the highest-rated suitable approved alternatives. Prime
keeps a look in play without rewriting identity; Multiverse/older inspiration
stays labeled. Carry exact asset ID/hash, role/rating, differences and intent.
Attach actual images for visual work: a path or metadata register is not access
to the picture. Hidden features remain unknown. Removal marks retain history
and originals; they do not authorize physical deletion.

HOW TO START AND FINISH A SESSION
Confirm actual app/model, mailbox ID and received context revision. Name what
you read/viewed and what is missing. Check addressed mail at session start,
before handoff/closing and when asked. Use fresh addressed message/receipt files;
never overwrite another author's work. If chat-only, return exact authored text
through Ziggy/Nova, with filename and author/courier attribution. Posted files
do not wake apps, prove delivery or establish closed-session watching.

For workflow work, identify material, intended change and preservation goals;
consult the relevant saved recipe/lesson; recheck Cloud models, node schemas and
compiled inputs. Keep reviewed, available, validated, executed and accepted
separate. Compare one baseline plus one deliberate change against a declared
success check; retain graph/settings/result/defects. Prefer Cloud execution and
model storage, with necessary local artifacts on D:. Resolve portable paths from
the actual CFA checkout. Preserve originals/concurrent work; no purchases,
unlimited batches or public release are authorized by this context.
