## 1. What Ziggy said, in substance

The top of the page is detailed and probably segmented correctly, but it is centred on the writings; below it, the storyboard's progression with images is Meta Mirror's only, and the bottom navigation is calibrated on Meta Mirror alone, while storyboards and story writings exist for other episodes and ideas. He wants both: a universal landing panel that reaches the targeted projects (the song, Meta Mirror, first) and the others. The Stage drop-down is not useful now (nothing has progressed beyond production, detailed design and verification) and he is not sure how it will be used; instead of a stage he wants to pick a story: Meta Mirror by default, since it is the first established and probably the first made, then storyboard one, two, three, the writer's storyboards, each showing its story panels. He asked whether the writings were made from the images (the writer's answer is in §4) and said that where they were not, the images we have must now be linked to the stories, because a story is easier to grasp with a visual representation; and he wants it clear which writings have a storyboard and story panels behind them.

## 3. The request, in the page's own terms (Nova's shape; the writer proposes only what the page needs to show)

1. **A Story selector as the primary control.** Per project, its options: the song storyboard (Meta Mirror's 50 panels; Hon's 36), then every `storyboardSequences` entry of the project (Hon's Grill Up draft; Lisan's Story 01 once intaken), then later stories as they come. Default: the song storyboard. Stage stays as a secondary filter inside a story, or as a badge on each panel, not as the first control. The Theme select and the From/To range apply to the song storyboard (they are built on panel order and lyric themes); a written story shows its sections (scenes) in order with its shots, and the range control hides.
2. **A landing panel at the top of the Storyboard view, both projects.** One card per story: cover (the `browseLayout.storyCovers` entry or the sequence's `coverID`), title, role, stage, the counts (panels; shots with a frame on disk; shots with a result on disk; shots waiting on a character reference), and the writings behind it (its `writerDevelopment` documents by association). Writings that have no panels yet (the series plan, the nine seeds, VALIS, Time and the Fall, the character notes) get a card in a "Series and seeds" group that says so in one line and names what they feed (the character packet). This is what makes it clear which writings have story panels behind them.
3. **The writer-record cards move under their story.** Meta Mirror: the scene cards, the cue map, the card deltas, the song alignment page. Story 01: the story text, the skeleton, the panel map, the three cue cards (the sandworm, the vault, the tower), the direction sheet. Series and seeds: the rest. Hon: the eight scene cards under Hon's song storyboard, as their associations already place them.
4. **Intake for Story 01.** (a) The LAG-1K frames named in the panel map (the story map's team picks; the folders' NOTES self-scores as prelim, not reviews; nothing accepted), into an album such as `lisan:story01-frames`, tagged as setting stand-ins: the place and its light stand for the shot; the figure in every LAG-1K frame is Lisan. (b) A `storyboardSequences` entry for Story 01 from the JSON: id LAG-STORY-01-THE-GLOVED-HAND; sections = the nine scenes; shots with their materials resolved from repoPath to assetID at intake, as the associations do for documents; `pendingReferences` shown as a visible placeholder ("pending: Angels") so the gap reads on the page; stage preliminary; timing null. (c) `storyboardMetadata.lisan.independentSequenceCount` becomes 1.
5. **Results tied to the shots they ran for.** The first clip's Flow return (FLOW-LAG-SW2-001) under S01-4.1; the Signal's two takes under the Meta Mirror panel they run for; later Gemini runs likewise, as `shotMaterials` already does for shot plans; every result marked preliminary, acceptance separate.
6. **Hon Dolo gets the same landing treatment** with what exists: the song storyboard, the Grill Up sequence and the eight scene cards with their 41 associations.

The nine seeds each carry an archive bullet naming the categories they grew from. The Hon scene cards were built on the storyboard panels by SB id.
