REFERENCES
Identify each actual attachment by its exact filename and role: identity, tattoo detail, wardrobe, setting, composition, starting frame or movement. Follow the role, not every incidental feature of the picture. Never invent a mapping from a bare picture number. Record upload batch + image number + exact filename if the interface labels attachments numerically.

Current working references govern continuity. Storyboards propose staging; outfit pictures supply clothing; historical designs remain inspiration unless explicitly revived. If references disagree, name the conflict and follow the user's latest decision. Do not blend different faces or projects to average away a conflict.

Use Ingredients/References when the selected video model supports them. Use Frames when a supplied image should set the beginning or end composition. For a single-frame route, prepare one coherent, reviewed starting frame first. A text instruction does not guarantee correction of wrong tattoos already visible in a starting image.

SHOTS
Default to 16:9, one continuous shot, one main action and one purposeful camera move. Maintain believable anatomy, contact with props, eyelines and direction of movement. Favor visible intention and natural breathing over random motion. Preserve fine tattoos, piercings and teeth through turns and expressions. Let collars and sleeves occlude artwork naturally. Leave short editorial holds where the chosen duration permits.

Start with one take at the duration supported by the selected mode. Keep framing, source and creative prompt fixed when comparing one deliberate change. Multiple alternatives require a specified small batch. Keep exact model, mode, duration, aspect ratio, output count and displayed credit cost with the take record; subscription access does not establish unlimited generation.

AUDIO
For music-video picture coverage, request no dialogue, narration, invented lyrics or added music. Any generated ambience is a temporary separate element. We will use the supplied song master in the edit. Replacing the soundtrack does not synchronize a visible mouth. A voice reference is not proof of exact waveform reuse, exact words or sung timing. Reserve exact lyric performance for a dedicated audio/visual synchronization experiment with an identified recording and trim.

HANDOFF
Return the actual download filename, project and scene/take ID, references used and their roles, effective prompt/settings, visible defects and known sound content. Keep the untouched original beside later edits. Do not claim a file is saved in CFA or a result is accepted without verification. Naming example: HD-S10A_FLOW_T01 or LAG-SC-28_FLOW_T01, with original download filename recorded separately.

REVIEW
Check identity at the start, middle and end; tattoo side/shape/text; jewelry count; teeth; hands; costume; motion; background coherence; camera; and audio. A pleasing motion study can remain useful even if it fails character continuity, provided that limitation is explicit.
