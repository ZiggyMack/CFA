A ten-worker Grok team and a single other-provider session are different configurations; name the resources and allowance rather than claiming a like-for-like speed contest.

WHAT TO LOG FOR EACH GROUP
Record project, manager/creative owner, worker/session ID, model if visible, category/range ownership, approved request count and exact input hashes. Record actual start/end and first-result times, queue/idle/restart intervals, active worker count, requested and returned counts, unique image hashes, failures/duplicates and review outcomes. Keep provider-reported quantities distinct from locally verified files.

Report raw unique images per wall-clock minute and reviewed usable images per wall-clock minute separately. Include rejected attempts, corrective rounds, QA and operator/courier effort. Record displayed/actual credits or cost only when observed; otherwise unknown. Capture throttling/refusal and stop conditions without inventing quotas or promising continuous unattended throughput.

QUALITY AND ACCEPTANCE
Character/component likeness (likenessRating) and scene craft (sceneQualityRating) are independent 1-5 image assessments. Strong camera, lighting or staging does not erase an identity defect. Keep both numbers, preserve strengths and target corrections. Never average the scores. Whole-scene readiness remains a separate B-or-better review with required ingredients, no critical blockers and verified direction/camera/lighting. Provider picks and successful file intake are not user acceptance.
