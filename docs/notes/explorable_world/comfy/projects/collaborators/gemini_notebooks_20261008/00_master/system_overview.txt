REVIEW LANGUAGE
A star is a favorite, not a winner. An export-basket selection is a chosen subset, not an approval. A shot plan is a draft, not a message sent or a generation started. Keep / Needs fixes / Mark for removal are triage decisions; a removal mark queues work and is not itself a file deletion.
Prime is the current working lane; Multiverse retains alternate creative ingredients. Multiverse and removal requests stay out of Prime handoffs. Historical references remain useful within their stated roles.
A working-reference request is not an approval. An approved working reference has a scope: scene, identity, outfit, detail or complete look. Storyboard stages are separate: Preliminary, Detailed design, Verification and Production. A source-panel timeline is order, not measured song seconds.
The saved review file, versioned manifests and source hashes make decisions portable. A new browser or notebook does not inherit local browser state automatically.
