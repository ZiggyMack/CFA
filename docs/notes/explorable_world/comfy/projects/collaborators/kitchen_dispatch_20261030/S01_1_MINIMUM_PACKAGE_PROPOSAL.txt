WHAT EACH PART CONTAINS, AND WHO MAKES IT
START_HERE.txt - CFA Nova derives a short entry point: project/story/shot/take,
delivery ID, writer-card source/revision/hash, selected provider/mode, intended
single output and input-upload order, what to paste, what to return, and any
unresolved binding. It points to the review contract, not a new copy of it.

TAKE.md - Claude supplies the single reconciled creative take and the review
contract already in 11.11. CFA Nova freezes the agreed revision, with source
path/hash and section-scoped author/specialist/review provenance. Include the
clip-relevant A-D context, camera/light/performance/background/start/end,
continuity, cue intent, visible locks and must-not-change requirements; keep
adjacent story context labelled as context. Resolve adopted deltas before
freezing: do not ship old and new contradictory instructions as equal choices.
The full storyboard and unrelated alternatives stay at their canonical homes.

PROMPT.txt - CFA Nova derives the selected route's copy-ready prompt from the
agreed take, repeating critical requirements. Claude owns creative intent and
core wording. Record any necessary route translation; a material artistic
change goes back to Claude for a new creative revision. The actual submitted
wording is retained at dispatch even if it differs from this prepared text.

media_manifest.json - CFA Nova records every packaged media file's exact path,
asset ID, SHA-256, byte count, source/derivation and allowed scope; separates
conditioning inputs from preparation/review-only references; and maps actual
required inputs to the selected surface's exposed slots. It records crops or
other derivatives and their own hashes rather than relabelling an original.
No filename, upload position or stage label alone establishes a binding.

run_record.json - CFA Nova starts from the existing evidence template. It
records the package/delivery identity, parent take/revision, source-card and
prompt hashes, intended action and output bound, destination, requested mode
and settings, pre-stated review criteria/reference to 11.11, and actual-field
placeholders. Use the relevant video/provider fields; do not force Comfy-only
node/mesh fields on a browser-video request. Unknown/unexposed values remain
unknown, never guessed. This is a not-submitted plan in the sealed request ZIP.
The archive digest is stored in the external dispatch receipt after sealing,
avoiding a self-referential ZIP hash. A later actual-run record is a separate
artifact linked back to the sealed request, not an overwrite of the request.

inputs/ - the actual consolidated frame/other media the chosen mode consumes.
references/ - exact evidence needed for preparation and scoped review. These
are available to the operator/reviewer but are not automatically attached as
conditioning. SB-01 is composition history with an obsolete teeth map; its ID
can remain in lineage without copying it into active video conditioning.

Site Nova owns the site's package assembly controls, current-take/download
link, readiness/gap visibility, result links and review history. Site assembly
does not silently author a creative take or turn text coverage into acceptance.
