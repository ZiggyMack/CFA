## Revision path
A technical correction or route change preserves creative intent and produces a newly identified derivative/run. A creative change returns to the writer against the take's exact revision/hash; an adopted change becomes a separately identified creative revision, with direction and prompts reconciled. A new prepared-frame hash rebinds the input and flags dependent packages; it is not automatically a new creative take. Retain prior packages, originals, observations and verdicts.
