## Proposed reusable contract
Each map starts with a small orientation block: **reader, question, bounded scope, current state, next action, source cutoff and owner**. Do not make the reader traverse the source register before finding what to do.

Then provide a question-led route. Each entry names:
1. The source and exact revision or section.
2. Why to read it now.
3. What it establishes and its limits.
4. Which newer rule overrides an older detail, if any.
5. Where the reader acts and what evidence closes that action.

Finish with a short state/evidence table, genuinely unresolved dependencies, and a source register. Retain history behind a clearly labelled branch. Branch counts and labels can vary by task.

Keep these axes separate: creative decision; authorship and section maturity; available/bound input; technical check; human review; delivery/receipt; adoption; acceptance. A document can be the writer's current take while still a proposal and have no prepared starting frame.

Reuse Nyquist's question routing and reading journeys (Map 0), source purpose (16), method/recipe distinction (10), valid-for/invalid-for evidence (9), owner/direction (14), and evidence-based transitions (19). These are provenance for the proposed format, not newly adopted production rules. [R5]
