## Story beat

The hinge of the whole piece. He has his cut; the crates are aboard; he has a clean way out and the kid's fleet is in trouble behind him. He tells the kid good luck and means it as goodbye. Chewie gives him the look. His hand is over the throttle and does not hit the gas. Then the two words that break his own rule, and the grin that pretends they did not. In the lyric the business boast and the sacrifice sit six lines apart; this scene has to make the audience feel the space between them without a word of dialogue, because the words are the song's.

What must be legible by the end of the scene: he could have left; he chose not to; he is embarrassed to have chosen; the copilot saw it before he did.

## Shots, in order

Seven shots. Cuts, not continuations, except where noted. Screen geography for the whole scene: the clean exit lane is screen-right; the battle light is screen-left. Chewie sits screen-left of Dolo. Dolo looks screen-right at his display; Chewie, from his left, also looks screen-right, at Dolo, who does not return the look. Both eyelines go right and land on different things; that is the point. The ship turns right-to-left.

Scene-only exceptions: none. Panels cited "for staging" or "for mood" are composition evidence, not reference exceptions. Exact paths and hashes for the A/B/D/F references are in `collaborators/storyboard_detail_20261009/01_hon/workshop_current_20261009/IMAGE_MANIFEST.json`; for the SB panels in `asset_review_20261007/storyboard_crosswalk.json`.

### S07-1 The cut (insert)
- Purpose: the counting motif returns. Same four warm-gold rectangular credits as S01B, now in or on a battered case; crates behind.
- Direction: a hand closes the case lid or rests on it. No face.
- Camera: locked insert, slightly high, case centered, crates soft behind.
- Lighting: cargo-bay practicals, warm; the credits are the brightest thing in frame.
- Start/end intent: start on the four credits visible; end on the lid closing or the hand at rest.
- References: credit design and hand contact from the opening prototype's reference D, `HD-DROP-36679ee9953b` (SB-03; props and geography only); staging from SB-19 `HD-DROP-d3f1eb0d0504` with its chip stacks replaced by the four credits. Wrist chain and dark band `HD-DROP-b18c4a123579` (SB-06) if the wrist enters frame, on the provisionally chosen character-right wrist as in the opening.
- Continuity: same four objects as S01B; no coins, no fifth credit.

### S07-2 Good luck, kid (insert, then profile)
- Purpose: the farewell to the kid without the kid.
- Direction: Dolo's hand cuts a comms channel on a panel; a channel light dies. Then a short profile of Dolo, eyes forward, nothing on his face.
- Camera: insert on the panel and hand, then a locked profile medium close-up.
- Lighting: cockpit practicals; the dying channel light is the only change.
- Start/end intent: start with the light on and the hand approaching; end with the light off and the hand withdrawn. Profile: start neutral, end neutral.
- References: face `HD-RETURN-6c8900223ee9` (lf_full, identity only); SB-22 `HD-DROP-825d9fd99e3d` for mood only, not its wet white shirt and not its face. Outfit: the opening's dark brown distressed leather jacket over the open off-white shirt, chest ship visible where the shirt opens.
- Continuity: cursive "eye" on the character-right cheek, serif "know" on the character-left cheek, neck blaster and smoke character-left. The kid is never shown.

### S07-3 Still has not hit the gas (frame B, the key shot)
- Purpose: the decision forming. The scene's starting still.
- Direction: Dolo in the pilot seat, body turned slightly screen-right toward a distress display, character-right hand hovering above the throttle, not touching it. Eyes on the display. One beat: the eyes close. One beat: they open, still on the display. No head turn toward Chewie.
- Camera: locked medium, pilot seat and throttle in frame, the unlettered display at the right edge of frame. The edge of the copilot seat at frame-left, its occupant out of frame (Chewie is there, as S07-4 confirms; the single-character proof simply does not show him).
- Lighting: warm cockpit practicals on the body; the display's cool light on the face and the hovering hand. The two colors are the argument.
- Start/end intent: start with the hand hovering and eyes open on the display; end with the hand still hovering, eyes open, after the closed-eye beat. The hand must not touch the throttle in this shot.
- References: face A `HD-RETURN-6c8900223ee9`; mouth B `HD-B-91ef00348229` only if the lips part (external-off: four upper gold caps on lateral incisors and short canines, white central incisors, lower silver/chrome, two plain lip hoops); wrist chain and band F `HD-DROP-b18c4a123579` on the character-right wrist, which is the throttle hand, so the chain reads (provisional blocking choice carried from the opening, not canon); cockpit staging from SB-14 `HD-DROP-d36a3316372d` (composition only) and from COCKPIT-R2-01 `HD-DROP-14f8eb9979b5` (the user-kept pilot idea, which still needs its grill and neck-tattoo corrections before it can be a reference).
- Continuity: throttle hand character-right, chain and band on that wrist, both as the opening's provisional blocking choice, not canon; no forehead dice; no iced grill; target one hoop per nostril, ear hoop and crystal stud per A, minor variation tolerated.
- Missing art: an accepted cockpit set with a throttle and an unlettered distress display at screen-right.

### S07-4 The look (Chewie)
- Purpose: the copilot sees the decision before Dolo admits it. Same setup and size as S06A, held longer, with Dolo's shoulder now in frame.
- Direction: Chewie turns from the controls, screen-right, to Dolo and holds the look. No sound, no gesture.
- Camera: medium close-up on Chewie, Dolo's shoulder and jacket soft in the foreground at frame-right. A silhouette-and-eyes version is a blocking pre-visualization only; it is not a shootable fallback, because an unfinished required character blocks the handoff.
- Lighting: as S07-3, from the other side; the display's cool light now rims Chewie.
- Start/end intent: start with Chewie on the boards, profile; end with the look held on Dolo.
- References: no accepted Chewbacca production reference exists. CHEW-STAGE-01 through 03 in `casting/gemini_cast_reference_map.txt` and SB-18 `HD-DROP-b443c1d21d74` are staging only; their ape-like anatomy is a recorded defect, not a design.
- Missing art: Chewbacca.

### S07-5 Swing her back (insert)
- Purpose: the order carried out. Hands on the levers.
- Direction: Chewie's hands throw the levers, one decisive movement, no hesitation.
- Camera: locked insert on the levers and both hands.
- Lighting: console practicals; the display's cool light on the fur.
- Start/end intent: start with hands resting on the levers; end with the levers thrown and the hands still on them.
- References: SB-23 `HD-DROP-e693f5f7bff1` for staging only; the hands must be Chewbacca's, not the panel's. Fur, muzzle out of frame.
- Continuity: the levers move screen-right to screen-left, the direction of the turn.

### S07-6 No extra charge (mouth)
- Purpose: the grin that pretends the sacrifice was a favor.
- Direction: extreme close-up of Dolo's mouth, neutral, then the smallest grin. Lips may part; teeth are occluded unless the grin shows them, and then they follow the rule.
- Camera: the SB-01 framing exactly, so the audience recognizes the opening's mouth.
- Lighting: cockpit warm, a touch of cool from screen-right.
- Start/end intent: start neutral; end on the held grin.
- References: composition from SB-01 `HD-DROP-725c38458fd8` with its obsolete dental map replaced; mouth B `HD-B-91ef00348229`; two plain lower-lip hoops.
- Continuity: external-off throughout the song.

### S07-7 The turn (S07B, exterior)
- Purpose: the consequence. The junky ship leaves the clean lane for the battle.
- Direction: the ship, on its exit vector screen-right, banks hard and comes around until its nose points at the battle light screen-left. One continuous move. No cuts inside the shot.
- Camera: wide exterior, locked or a slow drift; the ship stays whole in frame.
- Lighting: the exit lane dark; the battle a cold glow at screen-left that grows on the hull as the nose comes around.
- Start/end intent: start on the exit vector; end nose-to-battle, hull lit by it.
- References: SB-21 `HD-DROP-88f20c203f5b` and SB-13 `HD-DROP-8896508767b4` for staging only; the ship's silhouette is CFA's open design question and no accepted exterior exists.
- Continuity: this is the committed turn the plan reserves for S07. S08 keeps its throttle-push card S08A (the hand finally hits the gas); any S08 exterior is a proposed continuation of this turn, not a second turn.

## Cut logic

1 → 2 on the hand (case lid to comms panel). 2 → 3 on Dolo's eyes (profile forward to the display). 3 → 4 on the eyeline (Dolo looks screen-right at the display; cut to Chewie, screen-left of him, looking screen-right at Dolo; same direction, different objects). 4 → 5 on Chewie's hands (from the look to the levers). 5 → 6 on the lever throw to the grin, the fastest cut in the scene. 6 → 7 the grin to the turn, held long.
