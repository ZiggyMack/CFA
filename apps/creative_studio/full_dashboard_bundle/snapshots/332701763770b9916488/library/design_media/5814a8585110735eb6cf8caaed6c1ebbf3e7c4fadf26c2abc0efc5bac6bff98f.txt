## Story beat

The ending the whole piece was corrected toward. He went to dinner with the wrong host and found out what the fine print said. He tried to be her knight and got put on ice; the joke about the title is the last thing his swagger can reach. Then the two things that are not jokes: he asks Chewie to get her home, and she says the thing nobody paid for. His answer is two words, and he has never said anything with less sales in it. The slab is shown last, after the farewell, as the plan proposes, so the picture ends on the price and not on the gag.

Constraints carried from the record: no implication that he knew the dinner was a trap or chose the carbonite; no literal death; his scoundrel voice survives (the two words are sung in his own bearing, not a different man's); the slab is a reveal, not a transformation on screen; Vader never appears before this section.

## Shots, in order

Seven shots. Screen geography: Vader at the far end of the table, screen-left; Dolo enters and sits screen-right; the chamber's cool light comes from screen-left throughout, so the dinner and the chamber share a light direction and cut as one place. Leia is screen-right of Dolo in the farewell; Chewie is screen-left. This geography departs on purpose from the existing dinner panel SB-07, which seats Dolo and Vader side by side at the near edge of the table with Vader screen-right; the departure is the proposal, and the dinner needs a new panel.

### S10-1 The dinner (frame D begins)
- Purpose: the trap sprung, told as composure. The host does not move.
- Direction: a long table, immaculate. Vader seated at the far end, almost completely still; one slow tilt of the helmet toward the door is the only motion. An empty place setting in the foreground, nearest the camera. Dolo is not in this shot.
- Camera: symmetrical wide, locked, the table's length running into frame-left where Vader sits; the empty place at frame-right foreground.
- Lighting: cool and even from screen-left; polished surfaces; no warmth.
- Start/end intent: start with the helmet facing down-table; end with the helmet turned toward the door, held.
- References: SB-07 `HD-DROP-f7c3d8374e68` is the board's dinner panel and evidence that the dinner exists in Verse 3; its seating (side by side at the near edge, Vader screen-right), its Dolo and its lighting do not govern this shot. Vader: VADER-ARMOR-01 (`VADER-EXP-01-black-armor.jpg`, the general slim concept; its role in the cast map is armor, material and lighting concept, not production identity); Thick Vader is a named variant with no verified reference, so which Vader this is remains Ziggy's choice. The Gemini candidate clip `references/Gemini Video/gemini_generated_video_58328bf1.mp4` is the user-directed S10A dinner reference and informs the setting; it does not establish continuity.
- Continuity: the empty place setting is Dolo's; it must match the chair he takes in S10-2.
- Missing art: the table and room as a set; an accepted Vader.

### S10-2 The guest (reaction)
- Purpose: the fine print, read on his face.
- Direction: Dolo in the foreground chair, the one that was empty. He has just sat. He looks down-table at the host and the swagger does not leave his face; it freezes on it. No words.
- Camera: medium close-up from the table's side, Vader a dark shape soft at frame-left in the distance.
- Lighting: the same cool light from screen-left on his face, with no warm source anywhere in the frame; the cockpit's warm practicals (which still mixed with the cool display and battle light in S07 and S09) are gone.
- Start/end intent: start on the half-smile he arrived with; end on the same smile with nothing behind it.
- References: face A `HD-RETURN-6c8900223ee9`; mouth B `HD-B-91ef00348229` if the lips part (external-off: four upper gold caps on lateral incisors and short canines, white central incisors, lower silver/chrome, two plain lip hoops); wrist chain and band F `HD-DROP-b18c4a123579` on the character-right wrist (provisional blocking choice carried from the opening, not canon) if the hands are on the table.
- Continuity: the opening outfit, dark brown distressed leather jacket over the open off-white shirt, chest ship visible; cursive "eye" character-right cheek, serif "know" character-left cheek, neck blaster and smoke character-left; no forehead dice; target one hoop per nostril, ear hoop and crystal stud per A, minor variation tolerated.

### S10-3 Night night (the tilt)
- Purpose: the one Carbon Knight joke that works as a picture. One beat only.
- Direction: in the chamber, Dolo's head tilts back, eyes closing, the smallest smile, as if going to sleep rather than being frozen. No freeze effect in this shot.
- Camera: close-up, slightly low, cool light raking the face from screen-left.
- Lighting: chamber cool; a faint warm glow from below and behind, the chamber's floor.
- Start/end intent: start with the head level, eyes open; end tilted back, eyes closed.
- References: SB-26 `HD-DROP-4aadd9404769` for the pose and framing only (its face, lettering and jewelry do not govern); A and B as above.
- Continuity: as S10-2. The fur coat in SB-25 and SB-26 is not the established outfit; keep the jacket unless Ziggy chooses a chamber wardrobe, which would be a new decision.

### S10-4 Keep her safe (the trusted look)
- Purpose: the third look. In S06 Chewie doubted him; here Chewie is the one he trusts with the only thing he was not paid for.
- Direction: Chewie, in the chamber's doorway or at its edge, receives the words. He does not nod. The look is steady and it is the first time in the piece that he is not reacting to Dolo's performance.
- Camera: medium close-up on Chewie, screen-left of frame, a sliver of the chamber behind. No Dolo in this shot.
- Lighting: cool chamber light; fur catches it.
- Start/end intent: start with Chewie turning to Dolo (offscreen, screen-right); end holding the look.
- References: no accepted Chewbacca production reference exists; CHEW-STAGE-01 through 03 and SB-18 `HD-DROP-b443c1d21d74` are staging only, their ape-like anatomy a recorded defect.
- Missing art: Chewbacca. This shot cannot be detailed past this card until that reference is accepted.

### S10-5 She said it (Leia, silent)
- Purpose: Leia's line is sung by Dolo in the lyric; he reports it. So her shot is silent: she says it, and we do not hear her, we hear him.
- Direction: Leia, close, looking at Dolo (offscreen, screen-left). Her mouth forms the three words once, clearly, with no sound of her own. Then she waits.
- Camera: close-up, her eyeline screen-left, locked.
- Lighting: cool chamber light, softened on her; she is the warmest thing in the room.
- Start/end intent: start with her looking at him; end after the words, waiting.
- References: SB-28 `HD-DROP-4964cf10eb47` for framing only (its performer is not the intended likeness). Leia: LEIA-LIKENESS-01 (`leia_source_crop.png`, the exact source-pixel crop, likeness input only) and LEIA-STYLE-01 (`LEIA-EXP-01-ivory-coat.jpg`, ivory wardrobe and hair styling only, per the cast map). No accepted Leia production reference exists.
- Continuity: her costume is a decision that has not been made; the ivory coat is a style candidate, not a lock.
- Missing art: Leia, as a production reference.
- Note for the lip-sync lane: her three words are mouthed, not sung; the audio for this moment is Dolo's lead vocal reporting them. The timed lyric map should mark the line as lead for his mouth and silent for hers.

### S10-6 I know (the two words)
- Purpose: the only close-up in the piece with no sales in it.
- Direction: Dolo looking at Leia (offscreen, screen-right). The two words, in his own bearing, quiet. Nothing else moves. No grin.
- Camera: the SB-29 framing, close, locked; cool light from screen-left.
- Lighting: as the chamber; a faint warm reflection in the eyes from screen-right, from her.
- Start/end intent: start with him looking at her; end after the words, still looking.
- References: SB-29 `HD-DROP-d4f916925c82` for framing only (its performer is not the likeness); A face; B mouth, which will be visible, external-off; lip hoops.
- Continuity: as S10-2. This is a lead-lane lip-sync shot; the ad-lib "I know"s that follow in the outro are not mouthed here.

### S10-7 Carbon Knight (the slab)
- Purpose: the price, shown last. A reveal, not a transformation.
- Direction: cold vapor clears from a finished carbonite slab bearing his frozen silhouette, hands raised, face held in the last expression. Slow. No one else in frame.
- Camera: wide to medium, slow push or locked; the slab centered; the chamber's cool light from screen-left raking the relief.
- Lighting: chamber cool; vapor catching it.
- Start/end intent: start with vapor obscuring the slab; end with the relief fully readable.
- References: SB-27 `HD-DROP-16ad386979c6` for staging only; the frozen likeness is a prop design that does not exist and must be made from A, not from the panel. SB-25 `HD-DROP-e397f8389a14` shows the chamber before the freeze and is staging for the room, not for this shot.
- Continuity: the raised hands match-cut to the opening's poised hands over the credits in S11; the chain and band are on the character-right wrist in the relief, provisional as above.
- Missing art: the slab as a designed prop; the chamber set.

## Cut logic

1 → 2 on the empty chair becoming occupied (the helmet turns toward the door; cut; he is in the chair). 2 → 3 on the cool light: the same light, the same man, a different room; the dinner ends with his frozen smile and the chamber begins with his head tilting back. 3 → 4 on the eyes closing to Chewie's eyes opening on him. 4 → 5 on the trusted look to her face: both are looks at Dolo from either side. 5 → 6 on her mouth to his: the answer. 6 → 7 the longest hold in the piece, then the vapor.
