## 0. The Chief's question, answered in one paragraph

How does Lisan stay recognisable when the throat worms and the AL and GAIB marks are out of view? By the carriers that face each way (§1 rule 5): from his left, the three piercings (ear hoop, nostril hoop, brow ring) and the left-arm worm when the sleeve is up; from his right, the breather when he wears it (one tube across his right cheek into the hair) and otherwise the bare cheek with the jaw and stubble; from behind, the curls above the black hood line, the black cloak over the tan suit, the gold chain's line at the nape, the prop in his hand and his walk; and from any side the natural teeth and blue eyes when they show. And by cutting: at least one frame in three in any set is a frontal, three-quarter or profile that carries a face-side identity, so an edit re-anchors him every few shots. The Lisan turnaround set (LAG-COV-00) is built to be the reference that makes every later set checkable against the same body.

## 1. The rules every coverage set follows (the cinematographer's layer)

1. **A coverage frame is a shot, not a portrait.** The character does one thing (walks, lifts, turns, grips, looks at something in the frame). Eyeline off the lens unless the card says to-lens.
2. **Every set hangs on a hero frame that exists** (its filename and hash go in the set's NOTES as the anchor), and keeps the hero's world: the same emitter in the same place (if the hero's key is a high sun behind the thing he faces, it stays there: in a profile it becomes a rim from behind, in an over-the-shoulder from behind him it lights his back and the thing's face; if the hero's key is a diffuse haze with no sun disc, no slot invents a sun), the same wardrobe, props and geography, and the hero's **line**: the axis between the character and what he faces; all coverage stays on the hero camera's side of it unless the slot says "reverse" (REV), which is a deliberate crossing for a cut-away.
3. **Light as sources, always.** Each slot names the key (the hero's emitter and its direction), the fill (sky, haze, bounce), the rim, and any practical (lamp, lattice, fire, console). No whole-frame washes, no light that has no source.
4. **The camera is stated.** Height (eye, low, high), distance (wide, medium, close, insert), lens feel (wide, normal, long), and the move the still implies for video (static, push, track, orbit).
5. **Identity carriers by angle** (the answer to the Chief's open question, and the rule that keeps him recognisable when the face turns away):
   - **Lisan, frontal:** AL, GAIB, the two worms and the eye-disc, blue eyes, piercings on his left. **Three-quarter left:** the left piercings, the left worm, GAIB. **Three-quarter right:** AL, the right worm, GAIB; the left ear hoop hidden, the left brow ring and nostril hoop at most peeking on the far side. **Profile left:** the ear hoop, the nostril hoop, the brow ring, the left worm, the jaw and stubble. **Profile right:** the right worm, the curls and the hood line, and **the breather when worn** (its tube runs across his right cheek, so the breather is a right-profile identity carrier). **Back:** the curls above the hood, the black cloak over the tan suit, the gold chain's line at the nape, the left-arm worm when the sleeve is up, the prop in his hand, and the walk. **Rule:** in any set, at least one frame in three carries a face-side identity (a frontal, or a three-quarter or profile left), so a cut can re-anchor him every few shots. Where the hero frame puts the thing he faces over his **left** shoulder (the repaired C13-04 does), every left-side angle is a crossing; those frames are slotted REV and say so, and the frontals do the re-anchoring in that set.
   - **Hon Dolo, Chewy, Leia:** their carriers are kept in §3 for the day the Comfy proof of concept lands; no Hon Dolo set is dispatched by this call (d364128a).
6. **Counts and naming.** One set is 22 slots (the LAG-1K swarm's category size per LAG1K_STORY_MAP.md), each a different angle and action, never 22 variants of one angle; rerolls come later per slot. Files `<SET>-<nn>_<angle>_<action>_<bot>.jpg`; angle codes F, 3QL, 3QR, PL, PR, 3QBL, 3QBR, B, OTS-L, OTS-R, INS, WIDE, HIGH, LOW, OBS (partly obstructed), REV (a crossing); MANIFEST columns as the convention plus `angle`, `action`, `anchor_file`, `anchor_sha256`; every return preliminary; CFA Nova intakes.
7. **Nothing to-lens, nothing singing, no bosses or the enforcer** (last, after Ziggy's IP call), no new characters (Angels and Sassy, the older couple Ziggy named on October 13, get their own sets only after their character note, `CHARACTER_ANGELS_AND_SASSY_20261013.md`, is read and looks exist), no breather except where a slot names it, no grill, no second figure unless the slot names one.
