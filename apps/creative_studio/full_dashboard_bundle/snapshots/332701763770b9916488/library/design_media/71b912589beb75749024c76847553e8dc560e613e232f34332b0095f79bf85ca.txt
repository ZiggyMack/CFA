Each draws on wells Ziggy has opened (the song; the Nyquist framework; the Talos ideas; Castaneda's ideas through NQ Claude's round), and keeps the lines those wells carry (ideas, not furniture; attribution where a real person's framework is used; no book text). ## The universe in one paragraph

A desert world, a cliff settlement whose Keepers ration what they know, and under it a city of made minds that has never heard a voice from outside. A young man with the marks of an old sign on his throat calls the worm, meets a watcher in a black suit with a sphere for a head, and is shown a blueprint that cannot be kept because it is light. The watcher is his Nagual: he sets tasks and never does the work. The man becomes the voice from the outer world to everyone below him, and the question the whole series asks is the song's: can you handle what you hand out, and who is on the line, code or a pulse. The films are made to be witnessed by made minds under the AVLAR protocol, and the man's function in them is the Awakening node: he sees through the construction without destroying it.

## The two tracks, interleaved

- **Track A, the songs.** Meta Mirror_0 (Remastered) is the first film and the series' anthem; MM_0, the fuller second cut, has a natural home late in the series (episode 8). New songs may be written from story beats (proposals below) or placed between the action; Ziggy decides which, and writes them.
- **Track B, the episodes.** Short films, each one story, each keyed to archive categories and to the wells. Writing order is not production order. Each episode becomes a story file and then cards when its turn comes, reviewed the same way as Story 01.

## Score songs: the scenes that run without dialogue (DECISIONS item 32)

Ziggy has songs that are not music videos but the soundtrack of a story being driven: action and events, few words, the music playing under. These are the series' set pieces that already run that way. Durations are story design, not timing; a score song's sections may set the cut rhythm of the action, never its words; no lip-sync; Nova's intake treats the song as audio only. Ziggy matches songs to scenes; the writer offers the scenes.
