## Story beat

Real life resumes. The song set the boast against the slab in its last chorus and closed on the mouth (S12-2); the epilogue goes back to the chamber and says he paid the price and survived it. A short thaw, then Dolo alone in his quarters: he sits at the mirror, the lip rings come out, the case opens, the iced grill goes in, the mirror gives him back his smile, and he turns to leave. He is getting ready to charge somebody again. The last ironic beat of the piece is that the man who paid is back in business, and the one mouth change in the whole piece happens here, on camera.

Why the thaw is here and not in the song: the crosswalk's note on SB-30 reads "Thaw/reunion is a source invention, not established in lyrics; may weaken sacrifice ending." My reading of that: inside the song it would; outside the song it does the opposite, because the ending stands and then the frame story takes it back without undoing it. Ziggy said yes to the thaw moving to the frame (DECISIONS section 3, row 5).

Constraints carried from the record: the song's mouth is external-off; the r4 iced anchor (`HEAD-HD-ICED-ANCHOR-LOCK-r4.jpg`, `HD-GRILLUP-40064d98db89`) is Ziggy's selection for this external-grill look, with the lip piercings removed for this look only, and that selection accepts neither the storyboard nor a rule for every grill variant; the ten quarters panels are drafts with a label-content conflict between the files named 02 and 03, a three-hoop count in panel 04 against two in panel 10, and identity, tattoo, jewelry and grill drift across 06–10; CFA's five repair priorities (`REVIEW.txt`) come before any of these beats is detailed past this card; the established Leigh hand-art reference and gold hand rings are to be preserved whenever hands are visible (the drafts drop them), while the exact hand-art transfer stays unresolved per the opening brief; no song timestamp is assigned to any of it; whether the informant returns as a witness is open (DECISIONS section 5).

Proposals, not record: the iced look's only scene is this one; the thaw is with Chewie and Leia does not appear (the farewell was the farewell); the mirror reveal is the last frame.

## Cut logic

E-1 → E-2 on Chewie's hold to the empty room: the hardest cut in the piece, from the chamber to home, no travel shown. 2 → 3 on hands on the bureau's edge to the same hand resting on it in the mirror. 3 → 4 on his look at himself to the fingers at the lip, the mirror left behind. 4 → 5 on the hoops in the fingers to the hoops in the dish. 5 → 6 on the hand leaving the dish to the hand opening the case. 6 → 7 on the open case to him looking down at it. 7 → 8 on the hand moving to the piece lifting. 8 → 9 on the piece below the mouth to the mirror. 9 → 10 inside the mirror, lips closed to lips parted. End on 10; or 10 → 11 on the smile to the turn, and end on the doorway.

Proposed last frame: E-10, the mirror. The piece began on a mouth (S01-1) and ends on one. The exit is a fine ending too, and it is Ziggy's call; both are written.
