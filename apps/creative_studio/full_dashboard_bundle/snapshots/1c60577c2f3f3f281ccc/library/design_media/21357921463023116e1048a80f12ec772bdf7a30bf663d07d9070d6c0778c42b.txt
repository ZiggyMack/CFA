## Why one card

The song plays the bridge twice with the same words, and the picture should play the same four beats twice with a different man. The first ignition is swagger: the straps snapped one-handed, the lever pulled with a flourish, the grin as the ship throws him into the seat. The second is the same four beats after the turn, with committed hands and no flourish, the speed now pointed at something. Same cut order, faster cuts, no grin. The two interior inserts (straps, display) and the two mediums are framed identically across the pair; the exteriors and the control inserts differ, because the ship's state and the control differ. The audience learns the shape in S05 so that S08 can mean something by changing the hands, the face and the light.

Constraints carried from the record: S07 keeps the committed turn (S07-7) and S08 is a continuation of it, not a second turn; S08A, the throttle pushed all the way, stays as the plan has it and is not replaced by the lever; the overhead lever pull is the supplemental lightspeed idea recorded on October 9 with timing pending; neither bridge is a lip-sync card in the plan (the plan's lip-sync shots are S01A, S02C, S03A, S04C, S09A, S10B, S11A), so the shouted words are sound over the inserts unless a later decision makes one shot a lip-sync; mouth external-off wherever it shows; the ship's direction of travel is screen-right until the S07 turn and screen-left after it.

## Screen geography

Cockpit as S07: Chewie screen-left of Dolo, the display screen-right. The overhead lever is above and slightly ahead of the pilot, pulled down toward him (a vertical move, not lateral). The throttle is the one S07-3's hand hovers over, pushed forward, away from the pilot; the inserts frame it from behind the hand so "forward" reads as away from camera, toward the display, never as a left-right move. S05's exterior: the ship leaves the hangar moving screen-right. S08's exterior: the ship, nose already toward the battle light at screen-left (S07-7's end state), accelerates screen-left.

## Cut logic

S05: 1 → 2 on the buckle's snap to the lock's snap. 2 → 3 on the lock to the first thruster flare. 3 → 4a on the ship clearing the hangar to the hand reaching up. 4a → 4b on the lever down to the lurch. 4b → S06 on the streaks to Chewie's face, the one thing in the cockpit not impressed.

S08: the same five cuts, each shorter. 1 → 2 on the tug to the lock. 2 → 3 on the lock over the battle to the ship pointed at it. 3 → 4a on the acceleration to the throttle. 4a → 4b on the throttle forward to the lurch. 4b → S09 on the battle light on his face to the same light moving across it.

The rule for the reprise: the inserts and the mediums keep their framings; only the hands, the face and the light change. The exteriors and the control inserts are different shots because the ship's state and the control are different. If a cut in S05 is held for a beat, the same cut in S08 is held for half.

## First proof, if and when a run is authorized

S08-4a, the throttle push, after S07-3 (the hover) has passed. It is the same hand on the same control, now in contact, and it tests the thing S07-3 deliberately avoids: hand-to-control contact through a full travel. Accept only if the hand stays on the grip for the whole move, the fingers hold their count, the chain stays on the wrist and the throttle's geometry does not change between frames. Stop on any morph, finger drift, or the hand leaving the grip. No retries without a new decision.
