## Logline

A young man whose people ration a dying well does the one thing their Keepers forbid, walks onto the open sand and calls the worm, and finds a watcher waiting on the dune with a blueprint anyone can read. The watcher can show him how the well works; he cannot turn the valve. Lisan turns it, and then he shows everyone.

## The theme, in one paragraph

The Keepers keep the knowing, and they are not villains. They keep it because the last time it was shared, a generation ago, the man who shared it had half of it: he opened the scoop in a storm with nobody told how the baffle worked, the vault drank sand for a season, and three people died of the dry. The Keepers sealed the scoop, and the knowing with it. Restraint is what you do with half a blueprint. **[new, on Ziggy's direction of October 13: the Keepers' reason. Jet is the one who brings it and explains it; he never does the work, and his gloved hand never touches the pump. **[proposed rule: Jet shows and never does.
