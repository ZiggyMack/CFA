BEGIN PROMPT
Analyze this external music video as a craft study for CFA. Attribute it to its
actual creator; it is not ours and does not become project canon. Do not infer
the creator's software, model, prompts, LoRA, lip-sync method, render engine,
compositing stack, frame rate or audio channels from its appearance.

First give an access statement: which source was actually available, which video
and audio intervals were inspected, what was sampled, and what remains blind.
Do not substitute captions, a title or an earlier summary for audiovisual access.
Record visible technical/session metadata only at the scope actually established.

Return a chronological sequence table. Use approximate ranges if necessary and
say so. A sequence may contain several cuts; do not call this an exact shot/cut
count without measuring it. For each useful range include:
- visible composition, framing and apparent camera/subject movement;
- performance or narrative action and the role of the cut/transition;
- light/palette and visible continuity of mouth, hands, teeth/jewelry, lettering,
  tattoos, costume and props; record unresolved or occluded details;
- audible evidence and its relation to the image, only if actually inspected;
- a concise emotional/symbolic reading, clearly separated from visible/audible facts.

Label every consequential statement: OBSERVED, PROVIDER/CREATOR CLAIM,
INTERPRETATION, PROPOSED TEST or UNKNOWN. Cite timestamps and the exact source.
Give practical scene descriptions instead of guessing camera hardware or models.
If creator credits document tools, cite those credits separately from our inference.
Do not import CFA's Comfy/Gemini experiments as evidence about this creator.

Mine at most three useful lessons from the same result. For each, name the source
moment, the visible device, why it might work, and what has not been verified.
Then propose one small test with: baseline; exactly one deliberate change;
elements to preserve; a success check; a failure that would disconfirm the idea;
and a finite scope. Keep character/song/approval boundaries intact. No test runs
or new approvals are authorized by this analysis. Re-reading your own answer is
not independent confirmation.

Finish with a short unknowns/conflicts list and the next useful check. Percentages,
shot durations and other measurements need a method; otherwise label them estimates.

If AVLAR is invoked, refer to the unchanged Nyquist S9 method. A technical breakdown
or visual-only spot-check is not a valid AVLAR session. State whether synchronous
audio+visual coverage, no blind segments, symbolic/emotional/function reading and
the user's reaction are present. Do not invent sensory access, personal reaction,
identity reconstruction, a PFI score or a Ziggy signature for an external creator.
END PROMPT
