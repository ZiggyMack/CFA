## Story beat

The first chorus named a price. This verse explains it: the bravado has creditors. He is still at the counter, still selling the ride to the woman with money, and every boast he throws is a bill he owes. Jabba's funding, the hunters, the name he will not let anyone play with, the captain's title he insists on, the junky ship that is the only thing he has ever been proud of. The verse ends on the ship proving itself, because that is the one boast that is true, and the card hands the eye back to the counter at the same angle it left, so the chorus that follows reads as the same man a little less sure.

Constraints carried from the record: the verse is sung to Leia (DECISIONS sections 2 and 7); she stays unseen in this section so that S03's glance is the first time the picture looks at her; mouth external-off throughout; no Vader; no Lisan crossover; the ship's design is unaccepted; the boss design is a shortlist with no winner; the hunters have no identity assigned.

## Cut logic

1 → 2 on money: her hand's stack to the lieutenant's ledgers. 2 → 3 on the boss's eyes landing on camera to the puck's light. 3 → 4 on the gloved hand leaving screen-right to the hunter standing screen-right. 4 → 5 on the helmet's turn to the light crossing the neck, the two "looks" at Dolo in a row. 5 → 6 on the neck to the man, same side, wider. 6 → 7 on the look screen-left for agreement to the crowd that gives it. 7 → 8 the hardest cut in the verse, club color to cold grey. 8 → 9 inside the same vector, the ship crossing to the ship banking. 9 → 10 the exit screen-right to the number, then the Maw, then frame A.

Verse rhythm: the first half (1–7) is rooms and faces, warm; the second half (8–10) is space, cold. The cold half is where supplied footage is most plausible, and nothing in the warm half depends on it.
