## 3. What is on screen [proposed]

Jet's cube (REF-02, the transparent cube with pale cyan edges and a gold centre, a candidate visual language per the record) unfolds flat in the air between them, or Jet draws the same figure in the sand with the bare right hand. Left: a small lit island, floor-lamp warm, crowded with tiny lit things (every object the settlement has a word for). Right: a dark sea that goes to the edge of the frame. Between: one gate, no wider than a man, drawn as the two doors of the song (panel 48) seen from above. On the rim of the whole figure, one bright point. When Lisan talks, the island grows noisier and the point does not move. When he stops, the point slides. The drawing is a live instrument, and it is lit only by itself and the sky; nothing in it glows the whole frame.

**Jet's lines (proposed; coinages paraphrased, no book text):**

JET: This is everything you know. It is small, and it floats.
JET: Everything else is this.
JET: The gate is where you want to go. Not wish. Want, with the whole body.
JET: When you stop the talking, the point moves. When the point moves, the world is put together differently. That is all the lessons are.

Lisan says nothing. Angels, who followed them out (CHARACTER_ANGELS_20261013.md), looks at the sand and sees sand.
