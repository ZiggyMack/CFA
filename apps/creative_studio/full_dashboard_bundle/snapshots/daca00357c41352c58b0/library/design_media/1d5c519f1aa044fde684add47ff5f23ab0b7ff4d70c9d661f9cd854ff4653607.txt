# The Cosmic Avatar (working title)

Ziggy's avatar, built out so he can live in the same world as Lisan Al Gaib.
Design ground truth: `refs/COSMIC-REF-CLEAN.jpg` (clean crop of the original). Lock rules: `DESIGN-LOCK.txt`.

## Name (suggestions only; Ziggy picks)
- **The Curator**: he collects and keeps whole universes.
- **Orrery**: named after the clockwork model of the solar system.
- **Mr. Nebula** / **Neb**: the street name the noir city gives him.
- **The Keeper of the Cube**: what the desert people call him, in the style of a Fremen title.
- **Ziggurat**: a nod to Ziggy, and a tower built to reach the stars.

## Look
- **Head:** a smooth glass head with no face, shaped like an egg and filled with a living nebula: towers of amber and rust gas against deep blue, slowly turning. A thin teal light runs along the rim. Whatever he feels shows up as "weather" in the nebula.
- **Body:** tall and slim, with perfect posture. Where you'd expect skin at the collar there is only starfield.
- **Suit:** a matte black suit, black shirt and black tie. Two small amber glyph pins sit on the tie, one above the other. They are his sigils and pulse when he uses power.
- **Shoulders:** segmented cyan LED trim along the shoulder seams. It works like a status light.
- **The fist:** his left hand looks like it's made of glassy space. He keeps it clenched because opening it is dangerous.
- **The cube:** a clear glass cube that floats beside his right hand and is never held. Inside it is a whole miniature solar system: an orange sun, planets and a far-off spiral galaxy.

## Powers
- **The pocket universe:** the cube is a real universe, small from outside and endless inside. He can step into it, store people or objects in it, and pull things out of it: starlight, gravity, a moon's worth of mass.
- **Orbit control:** things near the cube start to orbit it, like sand, rain or bullets. He can slow, stop or redirect them.
- **The star-fist:** his left fist holds a collapsed star. One punch is a small supernova, so he almost never throws it, and when he does it costs him.
- **Wayfinding:** he can read the stars, so he always knows where (and roughly *when*) he is. He can open short "fold" doors between dark places.
- **Limits:** the cube must stay within arm's reach. If it cracks, the universe inside starts to die. Using big power drains color from his nebula until it goes grey, and he has to "rest in the dark" to refill.

## Personality
Calm, precise and dry, like a gentleman detective who happens to be older than most stars. He talks rarely and briefly, but his nebula gives his mood away: it flares gold when he's amused and goes stormy red when he's angry. He's curious about humans, especially people who believe they have a destiny. He sees himself as a caretaker, not a conqueror, but he collects things (moments, songs, lost worlds) and has a hard time letting them go.

## How he relates to Lisan Al Gaib
- **The mirror:** Lisan's world is full of mirrors and prophecy. The Avatar is a mirror that shows the sky instead of a face. When Lisan looks into the helmet, he sees possible futures, not himself.
- **Sand and stars:** Lisan commands the worms and the desert below; the Avatar commands the sky above. They meet at night on the dunes, where the galaxy is brightest.
- **Dynamic:** part mentor, part rival, part uneasy ally. He shows up unannounced, offers Lisan a look inside the cube (a world where the prophecy went differently) and leaves the choice to him. Lisan doesn't fully trust him and calls him "the Faceless", but he keeps coming back for answers.
- **Story hooks:** 1) The Avatar is hiding a dead desert world in the cube, and Lisan wants it restored. 2) A sandworm swallows the cube, and the two have to go in together. 3) In the noir city, the Avatar hires Lisan for a job he can't do himself: one that requires a face.
