Conventions. The 12 song sections S01 to S12 are the structure (TXT header lines 1, 8, 21, 27, 40, 44, 50, 71, 74, 80, 102, 108 of `Hon_DoLo.txt`); the prologue and the epilogue are their own sequence contexts and take no position inside the song. Ziggy's six browsing groups (Pay me first 6, Debt and danger 5, The ship and the crew 6, Turn her around 6, The price of love 7, I know 6) are browsing groups, not a structure, and appear here only to say where each group's panels landed. A panel's role is the card's wording, abbreviated to one label from a closed list (composition, staging, idea, framing, mood, repurposed, precedent, concept, evidence, layout, legibility, continuity, reference D, reference E, text-described, wrist reference F, anchor, named only); the card's wording follows in the parenthesis, and where the label is this crosswalk's and not the card's the parenthesis says so ('continuity' for SB-05 at S02-7 and for SB-25 at S10-3, each from the card's Continuity line; the r4 anchor's label 'anchor' at E-6 and E-8 stands for the card's words 'for the grill's shape' and 'for the piece', at E-9 for a listing with no role stated, at E-10 for 'governs the look'). The reviewed opening prototype's attachments D and E are SB-03 and SB-04; SB-02 is text-described there. The wrist chain and band reference F is SB-06's asset id and is named in most Dolo shots as an identity reference; the crosswalk counts SB-06 as a panel use only where a card lists it among the shot's panels (S02-6, S07-1). The epilogue's quarters-board ids (SB-GRILL-01 to SB-GRILL-10, two filename ids the card uses for the transposed panels, the r4 anchor `HD-GRILLUP-40064d98db89`) are listed as the card gives them and are not among the 36 panels. Lyric cues: where the card quotes a cue, the cue is quoted word for word with its TXT line; where the card quotes none but the shot title is a lyric phrase with one determinable line, the line follows the title's lyric words and the cell says so; otherwise the section range stands. Source make-or-have is TBD per shot and never bends a shot (DECISIONS section 8). Where a card still carries a hold that a later decision settled, the row says settled and cites the section. Art state: existing where a panel stands behind the shot and the card names no missing art; partial where a panel stands behind it but the card still names missing art, or gates the shot on an unaccepted frame; missing where no panel stands behind it (a graded Falcon frame is noted as staging only and does not change the state).
