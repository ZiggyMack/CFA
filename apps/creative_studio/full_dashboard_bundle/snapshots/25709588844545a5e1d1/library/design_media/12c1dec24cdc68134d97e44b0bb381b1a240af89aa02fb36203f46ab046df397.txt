## Story beat

The song opens inside the mirror. A voice from the outer world calls through it, and the inner world answers with a question: can you hear us, and who is asking. Ziggy's own reading, recorded this hour: the outer world is calling for whoever could speak to the times; the inner world is the capacity, from inside yourself, to hear it. And the mirror is not only a reflection: "a way to portal into different scenarios and different people". So the chorus is a sequence of doors. The "….who?…." calls open the first two, the rooftop (03, line 8) and the kitchen (04, line 10); "— but Who cares,  who cares?" opens the dune (05) and "Do you? Do you Dare?" the cliff (06). The mirror tunnel is the door itself, and the gilded mirror on the wall is where the question is finally asked straight: who's most meta of all.

The song returns to this room twice more, in what Ziggy calls the mini chorus: not the full chorus but "almost like a mini chorus because it's still sung the same", giving "a second beat as they go into another verse". The rule for the returns is Hon Dolo's: the same picture, one change each time. The first return (the Refrain) carries the whispered question in Lisan's own voice; the last return (the Final Refrain) carries it in a voice that is "almost robotic and deep", which the writer gives to Jet Black, the candidate Ziggy named under his leeway.
