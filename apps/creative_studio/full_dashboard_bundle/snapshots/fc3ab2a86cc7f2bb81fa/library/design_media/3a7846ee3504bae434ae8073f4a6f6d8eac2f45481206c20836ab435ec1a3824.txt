## 1. The verse as one thought

The chorus named a price; the verse explains it. The bravado has creditors: the boss who funded him, the hunters who want him, a name he will not let anyone play with, a title he insists on, and the junk ship that is the only thing he has ever been proud of. He is still at the counter the whole time, still selling the ride to the woman with money; every cut-away is a bill, and the eye returns to the counter at the end at the angle it left, so the second chorus reads as the same man a little less sure. The first half (S02-1 to S02-7) is rooms and faces, warm; the second (S02-8 to S02-10) is space, cold, and the one boast that is true. The words are a guide: the verse crowds its creditors into one line (TXT line 11), so the pictures take them one at a time and let the words run across the cuts.
