## 1. The rule of three

Every camera position that is bound to a shot (priority proof or needed in v001) is generated three times, as three honest variants of the same brief (same axis, light state and backing; the maker varies composition, dressing and light within the brief, never the geometry), so the director picks one of three instead of accepting the first. Exploratory positions stay at one. Filenames carry the position, the light state and a variant letter (for example TCM-SET-IMPALA-L42__P03-LS01-a.jpg, -b, -c), L-numbers continuing as before; the index gains a column variantOf naming the position and light state the three share. Unpicked variants are archived with the family, never deleted.

## 3. How the director sees it

Per family, one contact sheet laid out with a row per position and light state and the three variants side by side, labelled a, b, c, with take 1 as variant a wherever it exists. The director marks one per row (or none, with a word on what is wrong). His marks are recorded as the author's selections, as the house was; the writer binds the picked variant to the shots the position serves; the other two stay in the archive. V_1 screens all three; her tags ride on the contact sheet so a miss is never the only candidate in a row.
