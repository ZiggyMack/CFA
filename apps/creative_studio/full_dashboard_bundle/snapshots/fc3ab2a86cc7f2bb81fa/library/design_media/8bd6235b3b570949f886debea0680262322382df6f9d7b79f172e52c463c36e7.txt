## Story beat

The ship is down on a dry world at sunset and there is cargo to move. Nobody sings. Chewy does the work: crates down the ramp, crates in the hold, the case of credits checked, and then, the job done, his hands go to the decks and the ship's lights answer him. Dolo walks away from the ship toward the business, as he does. It is the cheapest story in the piece to prove on video, because it is one character doing one physical thing per shot, and it gives the music video's three recurring Chewy images (the boards, the look, the fader) a life outside the song.

No bosses, no enforcer, no Leia: Ziggy wants the bosses handled with special care for IP reasons, so this set stays with the crew.

**Refinement applied (2026-10-12, mailbox 98220a40):** every output from these frames enters Preliminary with no per-image gate; the accepted foundations (face, mouth, wrist, the DJB sheet) do not approve the new scene; each output gets separate Complete look, Scene, Details and Outfit notes, never averaged. Lighting per frame is stated as sources below: W-1 and W-2 the low sun as the key with the hull in shadow; W-3 the hold's practicals and one cold spill; W-4 the credits' own glow as the key on his face; W-5 the console's small lights (and, in the C20 source, the corridor neon, which is the continuity defect recorded there). The actions chosen avoid body-to-object contact physics except the crate carry, which is a hold, not a climb.
