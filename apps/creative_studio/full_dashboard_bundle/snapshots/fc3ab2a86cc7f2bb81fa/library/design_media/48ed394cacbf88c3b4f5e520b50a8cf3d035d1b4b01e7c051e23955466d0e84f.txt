## What a writer takes from the diff

Set 2 is the fuller text: it foreshadows "Under the lies" after the Chorus, adds a third "who cares" return inside Verse 1, answers the mirror with "I am the voice of y’all" instead of a whisper, and ends on the devil's eyes. Set 1 is the leaner one and ends on the hook. Which is the master for timing is Ziggy's and CFA Nova's (the open questions, below); the cards are written so that either works, and the two-doors card already carries both endings as its two candidate last frames.
