CFA CHARACTER HEAD REFERENCE STANDARD

Adopted by Ziggy October 9, 2026. Reuse across projects; character identity stays separate.



THE THREE LINKED REFERENCES

1. HEAD LIBRARY: the selected character identity, hair, face/neck artwork, jewelry and mouth states across useful angles. Use a consistent head-and-shoulders crop, plain black tee or suitable neutral collar, neutral grey background and steady lighting. Keep relevant neck details visible. This is not a cut-off floating head and does not establish unseen rear anatomy.

2. MOUTH DETAIL LIBRARY: close-ups for tooth layout/materials, lips and rings. Match each head to the accepted mouth detail/revision. Macro shots do not replace the whole face or validate speech timing.

3. OUTFIT REFERENCE: one clear full-body hero view per outfit as the default. Link it to the character/head revision and outfit parent. Add garment, accessory or prop angles only when needed; do not repeat the entire head library for every outfit. Gun/no-gun views stay children of one outfit.

HOW TO RECEIVE AND REVIEW

Keep original files, filenames/source aliases, actual hashes, dimensions and generator metadata. Register new bytes once; variants and views share one character parent. Do not mark a provider progress claim as receipt.

Bind each received file to characterID + headRevision + mouthStateID + view + headPitch + grillState; also retain outfit/prop state, head pitch, parent macro/identity IDs, provider label, observed pose and take ID. The semantic coverage key is separate from byte deduplication. Do not use invented IDs for unavailable pictures.

Validate files and hashes, then assess face/hair, correct tattoo text and side, tooth count/material/length, lip rings, nostril placement, requested articulation and background/framing. Track pass, defect, occluded or unknown independently. Hidden teeth behind closed lips are occluded, not proved correct or missing. Keep F/V contact and difficult lip-bite poses explicit.

Received, decoded, reviewed, selected by user and accepted for a specific purpose are different states. Maintain an overview/contact sheet and a coverage/gap matrix. Preserve rejected takes and earlier revisions as history. Existing outfits with obsolete mouths need a targeted review/correction pass; do not delete all older imagery or treat it as current head authority.



HOW TO USE THE LIBRARY

Select a small, relevant, compatible subset for the actual tool: matching head angle/state, mouth detail, outfit and any needed pose/scene/prop reference. Record exact attachments and roles. Do not assume a model consumes all 130 at once, that a head can be pasted onto any body unchanged, or that still consistency proves video consistency.

Prepare a consolidated, reviewed starting image with the chosen head/mouth revision and outfit where the route requires one. Then test a short representative shot and inspect motion, closure, occlusion and jewelry continuity. Preserve the existing finite mouth proof and workflow-intake procedure; no new benchmark budget is implied.
