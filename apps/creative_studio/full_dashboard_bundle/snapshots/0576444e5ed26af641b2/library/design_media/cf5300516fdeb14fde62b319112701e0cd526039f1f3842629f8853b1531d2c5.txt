## 1. What the answers change

1. **Present day, not period-neutral.** The film is set now; every cell phone is a smartphone. Wherever v001 says "period-neutral" read "present day: nothing that dates the frame to another decade, no legible text". Flat screens are allowed wherever the script places one (the desk computers at 418, the bar television at 1534, the hospital monitors), always dark or with an illegible glow. Phone screens remain separately prepared graphics composited later (as the highway sign at 1370 already is), so every phone in a set still is face down or dark. Background traffic, parked cars and the optional emergency van read as present-day vehicles, unmarked and unlettered. The no-legible-text rule stands everywhere. v001's screening notes that set an alternate aside as "modern" or "contemporary" lose that reason; nothing in this round changes for it.
2. **The car has a new identity.** The author replaced the sedan the script names at line 96 with a present-day unmarked full-size American performance sedan (his model name is in the interview file; provider-facing text keeps "a dark unmarked sedan"). Section 2 gives the identity. The family keeps its id TCM-SET-IMPALA and its fourteen positions, light states and filenames. Its take 1 (31 stills, L11 to L41, delivered to D: on 2026-10-31 before the answer came) stands as take 1; the identity reads in fifteen of those stills, and section 2 asks for a fifteen-still take 2 of exactly those, which keeps the round under the Chief of Staff's cap.
3. **The agency is settled.** John, Diego, the captain and every uniformed officer are Massachusetts State Police (the script's State police at 525 and 1809); the two agents are FBI, as the script names them. Set stills stay unlettered (no seal, no badge, no lettering); where a marked police vehicle is in frame it is a present-day police SUV in a two-tone blue livery with its lettering illegible (section 3). The precinct exterior (TCM-SET-PRECINCT-EXTERIOR) is a state police facility; v001 already draws it without any crest, lettering, flag insignia or agency marking, and that stands.
4. **The flashback's year.** The boy was fifteen and John is thirty-three, so TCM-SET-CHILD-HOME sits eighteen years before the present: a late-2000s domestic interior with nothing dated in frame. v001's "neutral era" default stands with that anchor.
