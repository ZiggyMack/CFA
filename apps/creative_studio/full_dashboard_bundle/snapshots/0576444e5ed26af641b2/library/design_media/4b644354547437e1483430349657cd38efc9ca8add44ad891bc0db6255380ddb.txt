## 3. Process (rolling batches)
1. Each reviewer lane (V_2 to V_5) writes batch01 (its first 3 candidates), freezes it, and reports it to Chief.
2. V_1 checks each batch01 against that lane's acceptance checklist. She writes `V1_batchcheck_<UTC>_v001.md` (frozen): PASS, or a list of the failed checklist numbers.
