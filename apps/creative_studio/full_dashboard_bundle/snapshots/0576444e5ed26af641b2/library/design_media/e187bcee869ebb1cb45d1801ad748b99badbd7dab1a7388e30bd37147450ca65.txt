## Story beat
The ship is down on a dry world at sunset, and there is cargo to move. Nobody sings.
- Chewy brings the crate down the ramp, and then hauls the lever that works the ship.
- Dolo walks off toward the business.
- In the hold, Chewy stacks and lashes the cargo, checks the case of credits, and fills the doorway when the job is done.
- Then his hand goes to the decks.

Each frame has one character action, and Chewy is the one working in every frame.

## Recommended first run: W-1 (the ramp)
W-1 has one character, one action and no face lock to fail on. W-2 (the lever) is the second clip. Run the rest only after Ziggy has reviewed the W-1 and W-2 videos.

## Cut logic
1. W-1 → W-2: the crate reaches the ground; cut to the hand on the lever.
2. W-2 → W-3: the lever comes down; cut to Dolo walking away.
3. W-3 → W-4: Dolo leaves frame; cut to the hold.
4. W-4 → W-5: the crate lands on the stack; cut to the strap.
5. W-5 → W-6: the buckle closes; cut to the case.
6. W-6 → W-7: his eyes drop; cut to him in the doorway.
7. W-7 → W-8: he steps through; cut to the hand on the fader.

That is eight shots, each with a single action. There is no dialogue and no song.
