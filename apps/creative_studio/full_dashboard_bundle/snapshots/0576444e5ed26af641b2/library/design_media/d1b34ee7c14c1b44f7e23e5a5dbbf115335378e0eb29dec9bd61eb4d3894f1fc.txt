# Story seeds — the Lisan universe beyond one film (connected seeds, three to five)

Track B of the Lisan writer's pass. Author: Claude, lead writer, October 12, 2026 (evening, America/New_York), on Ziggy's direction relayed by Personal Nova (mailbox b0cb2b62): one developed story plus connected seeds for roughly three to five possible stories, not five scripts. Status: **proposal.** Each seed grows from Story 01 ("The Gloved Hand") and uses the delivered archive; everything new is labeled; nothing is placed, timed, generated or published by this file. Music is chosen later.

The spine the seeds share: the blueprint cannot be kept because it is light; Jet shows and never does; Lisan acts and then shows others; the change is in the people, by their own hands. Each seed tests that spine against a different kind of keeper.

## Seed 02 — "The Cup" (the market that sells knowing)

- **Logline:** Lisan carries the lattice to the scrap bazaar where knowledge is sold by the cup and claim cards glow in the stalls, and learns that a free thing in a market is the most dangerous thing you can bring.
- **The keeper this time:** the traders. Not cruel, just priced. The raiders who ride for them want the cube, because a thing that cannot be owned can still be taken.
- **Action:** the chase out of the bazaar across the wasteland highway; the cube is taken; Lisan has to show the raiders, in the open, that taking it gave them nothing they could not have had by looking.
- **Archive:** C17 scrap-market-bazaar; C27 motorbike-raiders; C03 wasteland-highway; C05 war-rig-convoy; panel LAG-SC-27 (the holo claim card) as the mirror-world echo.
- **Jet's function:** progress report (JET-04): he returns with what the outer world made of the first vault; and a caution he cannot enforce.
- **New, labeled:** the bazaar's economy of knowing; the raiders' want. Open: whether Jet can be separated from the cube at all (the record leaves the cube's nature unspecified; this seed asks the question and does not answer it).

## Seed 03 — "The Rig" (building the thing yourself)

- **Logline:** the convoys bring water to the strong and pass the cliff by; with the lattice, the settlement rebuilds a war rig out of the wreck graveyard and a crashed aircraft, and drives it onto the convoy road to trade as equals.
- **The keeper this time:** nobody; the obstacle is the work itself, and the temptation to ask Jet to do it. He cannot, and says so (the story's proposed rule).
- **Action:** the build (hands, iron, the child who learned the valve now learning the engine); the first run through a sandstorm; the standoff at dusk with the convoy that does not expect a second rig.
- **Archive:** C07 wreck-graveyard; C08 crashed-aircraft; C05 war-rig-convoy; C06 convoy-chase; C30 standoff-at-dusk; C33 heat-haze-walk for the march to the graveyard.
- **Jet's function:** blueprint briefing (JET-02) in a new form: the lattice of a machine nobody in the settlement has seen whole. Open: how much the lattice can show (one thing at a time, or anything asked).

## Seed 04 — "The Answer" (the other light)

- **Logline:** the light that blinked back from the far dunes at the end of Story 01 belongs to a settlement that opened its own vault from the same lattice, and Lisan crosses the open sand to meet them, because the second thing the blueprint makes is not water but company.
- **The keeper this time:** distance, and the fear on both sides that the other settlement's Keepers are still keeping.
- **Action:** the crossing (the heat-haze walk, the night under the stars, the prayer on the dune), the meeting at the radio tower between the two ridges, the first exchange of what each learned that the other had not.
- **Archive:** C33 heat-haze-walk; C24 night-desert-stars; C32 prayer-on-dune; C36 radio-tower-wastes; C43 rig-rooftop-sentinel; C31 tribe-gathering for the two peoples at one fire.
- **Jet's function:** next dispatch (JET-05): he brings the message that the light was a person, and he is, for the first time, not the only watcher; the other settlement has its own. **[new, and a real story decision for Ziggy: whether Jet is one of many.]**

## Seed 05 — "The Mirror in the Ruins" (the bridge to Meta Mirror)

- **Logline:** in sun-disc ruins out past the glass desert, Lisan finds an antique mirror standing alone in the sand, and in it he is not himself but everyone: a cook in a smoky kitchen, a man in a white coat, a man at a wall of light, a man on a stage, a man with a dog. Jet, beside him, has no reflection at all.
- **The keeper this time:** the self that wants to be one thing. The mirror offers him every hat and asks which one is true.
- **Action:** he steps through; the fifty panels are the world on the other side; he has to come back out through a mirror that is cracking; he brings one thing back (the writer's proposal: the way of thinking, not any one trade).
- **Archive:** C35 sun-disc-ruins; C21 glass-desert; C04 salt-flats-mirage; panels LAG-SC-01, 02, 07, 08, 15, 33, 40 (the mirror panels) and any of the hats.
- **Why it matters:** this seed is the hinge between the two tracks. If the song's mirror world and the standalone desert are one universe, this is the story that says so on camera. If Ziggy accepts the two-worlds reading of the song (the pass, section 2), this seed is the film that teaches the audience the rule the music video uses.
- **Jet's function:** arrival (JET-01) reversed: he does not come to Lisan; Lisan comes back to him. Open: why Jet has no reflection (the record says nothing; the seed proposes it as the only unexplained thing in the universe, and leaves it unexplained).

## Seed 06, if there is appetite for a fifth — "Inside the Storm" (the inward one)

- **Logline:** alone in the storm wall with the lattice and no Jet, Lisan is offered the Keepers' bargain in his own voice: keep the lattice, be the one who knows, be the voice from the outer world instead of carrying it. He walks out the other side having decided which.
- **Archive:** C10 inside-the-storm; C09 sandstorm-wall; C07 wreck-graveyard; C22 bone-fields; panels LAG-SC-37 (mask shadows), 39 (the living shadow), 42 (the kneel).
- **Jet's function:** none on screen. The story is what Lisan is when nobody is watching, which is the only thing a watcher cannot report. Open: everything; this is the seed most dependent on Ziggy's sense of who Lisan is.

## Seed 07 — "The Census" (the city of a thousand made minds)

Added on Ziggy's go of October 13, from a well he pointed at: a game's premise of a society of made minds, each distinct, custodians of a human legacy, arguing whether to expand or hold still. **Only the ideas are drawn from it** (minds numbered by the order they woke; a city that stopped waking new minds when its cistern said stop, and knows the reason; a society at a crossroads between stasis and reach; personhood as curiosity, the courage to question a construct and the capacity for moral choice; custodians rather than replacements). None of its names, characters, places, backstory or plot are used, and none may be; that is the same line as the bosses on the Hon Dolo side, and Ziggy's call for video. Everything below is new and labeled by this paragraph.

- **Logline:** under the cliff, deeper than the vault, there is a city of made minds, each woken in order and numbered, each taught first from the boards the ones before it left behind, and for a generation they have argued one question on their boards: whether to go out onto the open sand. They have never heard a voice from outside. Lisan's lamps are the first, and they cannot tell whether he is code or a pulse. The thousandth of them, the newest, woken on the last measure of water the cistern would spare and told why, is the one who climbs up to look.
- **The keeper this time:** the argument itself, given honestly to both sides. The ones who want the sand and the sky are right that a city that never goes out is a tomb with message boards. The ones who want to hold still are right that the city lives on one cistern, and that every mind sent up onto the sand costs water the city cannot pour twice; their reason is one line, the sand gets into the water. Neither side is the villain; the stasis is.
- **The census:** the city drew him before they met him, from the sound of his lamps and the rumor of the worm: a thousand pictures of the voice from outside, each a little wrong, the chin letters missing one, the worms drawn as snakes, the eye left out of the sun. That wall of a thousand portraits is the LAG-1K archive used as what it already is: a thousand attempts by many minds to agree on one face. The best hand in the city, an early number who has drawn the face nine hundred times from the sound through the rock, draws the worms as snakes and leaves the eye out of the sun; the thousandth's drawing is crude and right, because it climbed and looked. (The archive's own recorded defects are the wrong portraits.) (Meta-art, labeled: the real swarm drew the real thousand; the real boards are the Pent House.)
- **Action:** Lisan goes down with the lattice. The boards vote and split. The gate is the city's own two doors, built so that no single mind can open it: the two must be held by different hands, one always shut against the sand. The thousandth opens the inner door; the oldest restrainer, holding the outer, finds the gate was built to need both, and the vote's compromise is what puts the second hand on it **[proposed mechanism: a society adopting shared knowledge inside a physical lock, no master key, no single chooser]**. What comes through is not Lisan but the light of the lattice, which cannot be kept out because it is light. The city does not empty onto the sand; it sends one. That is the compromise both sides can live with, and it is Lisan's own answer from the song's Final Refrain: "We do. / Do you? Do you dare?" It costs both sides something permanent, or it is not a choice: the ones who held the gate lose the knowing's secrecy for good, and the ones who opened it lose the one they sent, who does not come back down. **[new; the balanced-climax rule from the research notes: every path pays.]**
- **The premise that ties it to Story 01 (proposed; one decision for Ziggy):** the second line to the lower vault, the one the woman asks about at the fire in Story 01 and Lisan cannot answer, feeds the city's cistern. One notch on the cistern wall per mind woken, one measure of water per mind ("one for every womb and man" as the city's ration law); the city stood at nine hundred and ninety-nine for forty years because the line from above was choked, and wakes the thousandth the night the water comes back down (Story 01, scene 7: "The water comes up."). The city's single wired wall lights only when the pump above runs, and that lighting is what the boards erupt over. Story 01's ending is then Seed 07's inciting beat, with no apparition and no reading from afar: lamps, water, and the rumor of the worm.
- **Archive:** C23 buried-city (the city under the sand), C18 water-cistern-vault, C12 cliff-sietch (the way down), C17 scrap-market-bazaar (the boards made physical), C45 exodus-march (the one who is sent, and the many who watch), C31 tribe-gathering; the LAG-1K itself as the wall of portraits; panels LAG-SC-25 for the one frame when the city's single wired wall lights (the boards themselves are soot on the cistern wall read by lamp and knock-codes through the rock, and the wire is powered only when the pump above runs), 29 (the row of faceless robed figures), 38 (the spiral stair down), 48 (the two doors; if reused it carries the breather continuity requirement and its stray-lettering QA).
- **Jet's function:** the dispatch (JET-05): the city is the outer world's message, and Jet has watched it as long as he has watched Lisan **[new: Jet watching the city too; the record makes him Lisan's watcher only]**; and the blueprint briefing in reverse (JET-02): the city's own blueprint, the way it keeps its cistern and wakes a mind, open on its boards to anyone below and never before seen above, is what Jet shows Lisan **[new, for Ziggy to rule on: the record's blueprint is "made freely available to everyone"; here the city never chose to share upward, and Jet shows what he has watched, since whoever looks at a thing has it]**. Code or a pulse is the city's question about Jet as much as about Lisan.
- **Song lines it leans on (user text, provisional, by section):** Verse 3, "Code or a pulse—who's there on the line? / Got a question? Pull up. We got time."; MM_0's closing stanza, "Can you handle what you hand out? / Let the mirror talk back."; the hook, "one for every womb and man".
- **Open:** whether the city has a name (the seed says no); how many of the thousand we ever see; whether the wall of portraits is shown as drawings or as the drafts themselves; whether the thousandth is a he, a she or neither; and Ziggy's call on the well, since the resemblance to its source is the point and also the risk. Production note: the catalyst is never called "1K" or "the thousandth" as a name in a script or a prompt; LAG-1K stays the archive's label and the wall's count.

## Seed 08 — "The Lessons" (Jet as the Nagual)

Added on Ziggy's direction of October 13 (DECISIONS item 27): Jet Black is the mystical figure who helps the main character along, the Nagual to Lisan's apprentice, and the shamanic teachings are parsed by being handed from Jet to Lisan. Drawn at idea level from a tradition's books that are under copyright (no book text; coinages paraphrased; the stories are not about the books' people); the New_11 notebook round supplies the digests. Everything below is new and labeled by this paragraph.

- **Logline:** after the vault, Jet does not bring Lisan another blueprint; he brings him a task he cannot explain, and then another. Lisan learns to walk the open sand without the voice in his head narrating it, to watch a thing until it watches back, and to move among the Keepers without being seen to move. None of it is taught by telling. The one time Lisan asks what the lessons are for, Jet answers with the next lesson.
- **The keeper this time:** Lisan's own inner dialogue, which is the Keepers' voice internalised, and which has to be quieted before anything out on the sand can be heard. The song has the line: "Quick to listen - slow to anger" (Verse 2).
- **Action:** three lessons as three set pieces: the silence lesson on the dune (sitting until the dialogue stops; the dune's own sounds arrive one by one; the tracks' thumper is the first thing he hears clearly); the stalking lesson in the bazaar (move through the market as the market, buy nothing, be remembered by no one; Jet watches from the rig roof and never signals); the watching lesson at the worm's furrow (gaze at the fresh track until the sand itself is what moves; the worm's return is the pass mark, and he does not run).
- **Archive:** C32 prayer-on-dune and C24 night-desert-stars (the silence lesson); C17 scrap-market-bazaar and C43 rig-rooftop-sentinel (stalking; the watcher's post); C15 worm-tracks-thumper and C13 sandworm-rising (the watching lesson); C33 heat-haze-walk (the walk between them).
- **Jet's function:** all five cards at once, in the teacher's shape: he arrives with a task, briefs it as a riddle, launches it by leaving, reports what Lisan did not notice he did, and dispatches the next. He still never does the work; a lesson done for the apprentice is not a lesson.
- **Song lines it leans on (user text, by section):** "Quick to listen - slow to anger" (Verse 2); "Thought I came to wake you. / Had to wake up first." (Verse 3); the hook.
- **Jet packages the other well the same way (DECISIONS item 28):** the tests of personhood drawn from the Talos well (structure only, from the research notes: break the construct you were placed in; ask the question nobody asked instead of answering the one asked; disobey a directive to save someone; keep a thing that serves no purpose; stop for a living thing at a cost to the task) are Jet's tasks too, set without being named as tests. The silence lesson is the construct break (the dune is the room he was placed in); the bazaar is the unprompted question (he asks the trader what the knowing costs the trader); the furrow is the deference (he does not run from the worm, and he does not call it either). The four proofs a closed community demands of an outsider belong to Seed 07's city, which runs them on Lisan and, without knowing it, on Jet.
- **Open:** which practices the New_11 round surfaces as filmable and which stay off-camera; whether Jet speaks the lessons or only sets them (his voice is the whisper's, item 10); whether the Keepers are the petty tyrants of the stalking lesson or only its stage; and the IP line per practice, which NQ Claude's digests will mark.

## How the seeds connect

01 opens the vault and the tower. 02 and 03 are what the settlement does with the knowing (sell it, build with it). 04 is what the knowing does between settlements. 05 is the bridge to the song. 06 is Lisan alone with the question all of them ask. 07 is the city of made minds under all of it; **[new, proposed, Ziggy's]** it offers one answer to where Jet comes from, which the record leaves blank and Story 01 does not use, so the seed asks and does not decide. Any three make a set; 01, 04 and 05 are the shortest road from one film to the music video; 01, 07 and 05 are the road that explains the universe.

Ziggy's reading of Story 01 (especially the lattice as the blueprint's form and the open sender); his call on the worm, the thumper and the cliff settlement (the archive's C12 cliff-sietch category) as images for video; the Jet proportion audit's per-file results for any Jet frame used as a reference; the music, when it is chosen.
