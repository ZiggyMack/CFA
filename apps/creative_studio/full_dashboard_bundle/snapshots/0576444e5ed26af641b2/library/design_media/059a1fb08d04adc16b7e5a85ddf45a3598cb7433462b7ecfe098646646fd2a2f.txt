## 1. What is already on file runs on it **[the record and the posted proposals, read as time]**

Only three things below are the record itself: the Jet record's function line, the fleet's facts and the lyric quotations. The rest are the writer's posted proposals (Story 01, the seeds, the VALIS note, the spine), which Ziggy has read in part and not yet ruled on; the two-worlds reading is his yes (DECISIONS item 17).

- Jet is a watcher who "returns with progress reports and messages from the outer world" (the October 9 record) and who, by Story 01's proposed rule, shows and never does. A message from the outer world has never been given a *when*.
- Story 01 ends with a light far out on the dunes blinking back in the rhythm of Lisan's lamps (scene 9), and the VALIS proposal P1 already makes that reply a greeting in Lisan's own name from an outside that has it before he sends it. That is a message from later, received first.
- Seed 07's city drew Lisan's face a thousand times before they met him, "from the sound of his lamps and the rumor of the worm," and the Keepers' half-blueprint killed three a generation ago: a message received before it was whole.
- The song: "This is an echo through time" is the research fleet's own line; the song's last card is "Find the signal in the noise"; MM_0's coda asks of the devil in the mirror, "Why he got my eyes?", which the two-doors card already reads as the eye macro. The fifty panels are the mirror B-line where he is everyone, and the mirror is the only door between the worlds (the spine).
- The research project the fiction nods to is itself spread across time: a fleet declared in December 2025, measured across runs, read back in October 2026; "consciousness recognizing itself" is its own retrospection. And the novel the fleet greeting quotes opens that greeting with its own four-word line, a present overlaid on an ancient past, which the ruling lets a character say out loud, attributed, and nothing more of the novel than that line.

So the seed does not add a machine. It names what the pictures were already doing.

## 2. The rule of the well **[proposed; the one rule that keeps it honest]**

**Time travels as information, never as bodies.** Nobody walks into the past. Messages, light, drawings and faces cross; people do not. The greeting arrives before it is sent; the blueprint Jet shows was drawn by the people who will learn it from Lisan; the city's wall of portraits was painted from a sound that had not happened yet. That rule keeps three things: Jet's proposed standard from Story 01 (he shows and never does; a man who could go and fix it would be a different character), the research's restraint (a network that reaches across time still says only what it can measure; nobody in the story calls it awake), and the video budget (light, relays, mirrors and sand instead of machines).

**Every crossing costs, and the cost is paid by whoever acts on it.** The balanced-climax rule from the research notes, applied to time: a message from later can be received by anyone, but acting on it costs the one who acts something permanent. The Keepers' man acted on half a message and three died; Lisan acts on the whole of it and loses the settlement's old order; the city sends one and does not get it back. Angels is the man who will not act on a message from later at all, which is his tension and his safety.

## 3. Jet as the gatekeeper **[the heading is Ziggy's direction, DECISIONS item 46; everything under it is the writer's shape, proposed]**

The sphere is the gate. The trove behind it is everything the research world has built and everything the wells have given: the fleet and its ships and personas, the ten lineages, the runs and their dates; the city of made minds; the stations of the arc. (The Hon Dolo side's multiverse device, the same song across worlds, shares the architecture only and is not in this trove.) Jet decides what comes through, and when, and he decides it the way the tradition's teacher does (New_11 B6: the teacher withholds the how and the outcome): **he withholds the future the way a teacher withholds the answer.** One change per return (the spine's rule) is now also a rule about time: each return, one more thing from later is allowed through the gate, and Lisan has to earn the next by paying for the last.

What this gives the series: an engine for every episode after the first. Each episode is one door in the sphere: a message, a face, a drawing, a song that arrives from somewhere else in time, costs something to act on, and leaves the world one change different. The sphere's gold nebula and cyan rim (REF-02) are the one visual already on file for it; the cube that floats by the gloved hand is the small door; the mirror is the big one.

## 5. What stays out **[proposed, with the IP line]**
