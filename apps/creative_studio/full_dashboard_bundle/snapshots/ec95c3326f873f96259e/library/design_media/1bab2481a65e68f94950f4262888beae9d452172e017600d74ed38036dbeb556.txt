## 5. What the rescoring must deliver, and who consumes it

The Prime master gets one listening pass that produces a **timed lyric map**. Ziggy's point about echoes and ad-libs is the crux: a doubled chorus, an echoed word and an ad-lib are sung by the same voice but they are not the same mouth on screen. The map needs three lanes.

| Deliverable | Form | Consumed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Section boundaries | start and end time for S01–S12, from the audio, not the old subtitle timebase | every card; `shot_plan.json` section times |
| Line timestamps | start and end per lyric line, within sections | the lip-sync cards: S01A, S02C, S03A, S04C, S09A, S10B, S11A |
| Lane labels | each timed line or word tagged **lead**, **double** (the doubled rap-chant in the choruses), or **ad-lib** (echoes, repeats, the trailing "I know"s) | the lip-sync cards decide which lane the visible mouth sings; doubles and ad-libs are sound, not mouth, unless a card says otherwise |
| The beat stop | the exact moment and length of the stop after the line "Man, don't start." | S07-3 and S07-4: the hover and the look sit in the silence |
| Tempo and feel | BPM and the half-time pocket; the "punch it" hits in both bridges | the ignition inserts (S05, S08A) and the cut rhythm of the choruses |
| Silences and rests | audible rests after punchlines | editorial; where a cut can breathe |

Proposed route for the pass: Google AI Studio, which already dissected the inspirational video for this project (`collaborators/video_studies/lil_vader_MwHocQFlw_c_20261009/`). The request text is in `PRIME_HANDOFF_20261011.md`; the upload and the run are an operator action for Nova or Ziggy, not something this folder performs. Whatever the model returns is a provisional map until someone with the recording in front of them spot-checks the section boundaries and the beat stop; a model's timestamp is a report, not a measurement.
