# Video prototype brief — the standard every Flow / Gemini request must meet

Author: Claude, lead writer. Date: 2026-10-12. Status: working standard for the writer lane; it applies the CFA production method of October 12 (`collaborators/PRODUCTION_METHOD_20261012.txt`), the Flow project brief (`hon_dolo_strictly_business/flow/project_brief.txt`), the Flow002 milestone recorded in the method, and the shoot-plan verification fields. It authorizes nothing. A brief that meets this standard is ready for CFA Nova to dispatch when Ziggy wants it run; Ziggy's gate is the actual video.

**Refinement of 2026-10-12 (the method's dated user refinement and the Flow 002 A/B review, mailbox 98220a40):** this standard is a completeness checklist for a brief, not an approval gate. New scenes enter Preliminary without per-image signoff; a production-ready character, outfit or tattoo foundation never approves the new scene made from it; the new scene's likeness and scene/action are assessed separately before anyone claims readiness. Review dimensions stay independent and are never averaged: **Complete look** (the character overall, independent of scene), **Scene** (context and action, independent of character fidelity), **Details only** (one isolated feature), and **Outfit** (character fidelity as seen in the outfit, separate from whether the clothing is desirable). Lighting changes must be physically motivated (existing emitters, local falloff, occlusion, shadows and reflections that follow moving surfaces), never a global wash. Body-to-object interaction (entering a vehicle, descending, contact) is a known weak point of the current route, so a brief asks for one simple action. Flow's Omni Flash 1.1 video editing runs at standard 720p (draft 360p); Veo 3.1 Quality does not edit video; the account's export choices are unverified; record the returned duration and frame rate rather than assuming them.

## Why a standard

The swarm has produced over 2,500 preliminary stills. Ziggy wants the best of them flowed into prototype videos with Gemini and Flow, and he has asked the Grok team to draft the instructions and me to review them to my standard. The method says each executable task needs exact inputs, a declared change, preservation goals, an output bound and an actual supported mode and model, with the displayed cost verified before a run and unknown cost or access as a stop condition. This file turns that into a form the team can fill and I can check in one pass.

## The form (one brief per shot; one shot per run)

1. **Shot id and story place.** The card's shot (for example S04-7 Chewie's fader, or the "Chewy at work" storyboard's shot 2) and the section it serves. No song timestamp; timing stays null until Nova's alignment passes.
2. **Source image, exact.** Archive, category, filename, SHA-256 from the archive MANIFEST, the Grok preliminary scores (L and S), my grade if one exists in `CLAUDE_GRADES_20261012.md`, and Ziggy's review state if any. State plainly: preliminary draft, not an accepted reference. One source image per run; a second image only as an end-frame review target, never as an invented conditioning input.
3. **Identity locks to preserve** (copied, not paraphrased): Dolo per `6c8900223ee9__lf_full.png`, the four-gold external-off mouth, chain and band on the character-right wrist (provisional), cursive "eye" character-right, serif "know" character-left, neck blaster character-left, upright chest ship, the brown leather jacket over the open off-white shirt; Chewy per the DJB sheet (dark gorilla-like beast, black over-ear headphones, gold neck chain, bullet bandolier); Leia per the likeness crop with the ivory coat and no deciding jewelry; the enforcer per DA-02 and DA-04, described as the original sci-fi armored enforcer. Name which of these are actually visible in the source frame; only those can be checked.
4. **Declared change, one action.** What moves, in one sentence, with the start state and the end state (for example: Chewy's hand pushes the fader from left to right; nothing else moves). No speech, singing, lip motion, cuts, tattoo redesign, costume change or added characters. One gentle camera move at most. Avoid actions that depend on body-to-object contact physics (climbing in, sitting down, descending stairs) in a first test; a lift, a push, a turn of the head or a walk on flat ground is safer.
4a. **Lighting, stated as sources.** Name the existing emitters in the frame (sunset key, console practicals, a lamp) and what they light; any requested change warms or cools those emitters and the surfaces they reach, with falloff, occlusion, shadows and reflections, and leaves unlit areas alone. Never "make it warmer" as a whole-frame filter.
5. **Sides and geography.** The card's screen directions for this frame (co-pilot screen-left of Dolo in frontal shots; display screen-right; hatch screen-right; cantina counter along the right-hand wall from the entrance; battle light ahead after the turn), so motion does not mirror the set.
6. **Output bound.** One clip; the model's native short length (Flow002 returned 6.0 s, 144 frames at 24 fps, 1280x720, with an audio track despite a silent prompt); eight seconds only if the selected model offers it; no automatic retries; no extra batches. Record the actual returned duration and whether audio is present.
7. **Mode and model, actually supported.** Name the provider and the exact mode and model offered on screen at run time (Flow002 used Omni 1.1 Flash). If the mode is not offered, stop. "Flow" and "Gemini" are not interchangeable: Flow is the video route; Gemini's image or video create tools are a separate route with their own bounds.
8. **Cost and access check.** The displayed cost and the current allowance verified on screen before Start; unknown cost or access is a stop condition. No purchases, plan changes, settings changes or bypasses.
9. **Review checks, stated before the run (not a gate).** Identity, lettering, teeth (where visible), wrist side and wardrobe at start, middle and end; no morph, duplicate, added person or set change; the declared action completed and nothing else; light behaving as sources, not as a wash; audible content noted; returned duration and frame rate recorded. Each output enters Preliminary with separate Complete look, Scene, Details and Outfit notes; grades are attributed and provisional; Ziggy's own scores and his motion, acting and edit judgment are final. A pleasing preview is not preservation evidence.
10. **Provenance.** Save the original output unchanged with its SHA-256 beside the source image's hash; a mailbox message to `nova` and `claude` with both hashes; any later edit saved separately. Arrival is not acceptance.
11. **Owner.** Who prepares (the swarm or me), who dispatches (CFA Nova, or whoever Ziggy names per provider surface), who reviews (Ziggy, the actual video).

## What the Flow 002 test taught (A and B, both Scene / Preliminary / Needs fixes)

Ziggy's attributed review of the first Flow edit: the outfit was perfect and the blue night aesthetic beautiful; the performer was not a sufficient likeness; tattoos were hard to see; the car entry and body interaction were physically unreal; the warmth edit read as a yellow surface wash rather than motivated light. The lesson for every brief here: start from a source frame whose likeness already holds (the swarm's shortlist, graded), ask for one simple action, state the light as sources, and keep an inherited source defect separate from what the edit changed. A single lighting diagnostic (proposed C prompt v2) is on file in `flow/video_test_002/USER_REVIEW_20261012.txt`; it is a proposal, not a run.

## What I check when a brief comes in from the team

- Every field above present; no "see image" where a hash belongs.
- The source frame is one the shortlist or Ziggy named, and its identity defects (from the category NOTES and my grades) are listed, not hidden by its score.
- The action is one action and matches the card; the sides are the card's.
- Nothing asks for lip motion, song timing, a second character, or a boss or Vader video before Ziggy clears the IP question he raised ("take special care with the bosses").
- The bound and the stop conditions are written down.
