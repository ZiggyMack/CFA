## 2. LIGHTING

- Key: a warm overcast desert haze from above, soft, no sun disc; it does not move or change colour.
- Fill: the haze itself and the pale sand bounce from below, on his face and the armour's plates.
- Rim: none from the sky; the fire gives a faint warm edge on his far side only (his left, viewer-right) when it flickers.
- Practical: the fire on the rig at screen-right (viewer-right; behind him on his left side). As in take one it flickers on the rig, the worm's lip and the dust, gutters as the rig goes under, and is embers and lit dust by the end; only faintly his far cheek when it flickers; never his near side, never a new source.
- Dust catches both the haze and the fire; no whole-frame wash.

## 3. CAMERA

The line runs from him to the off-screen teammates up to his right (viewer-left, above the frame's left edge); the camera stays in front of him, on the maw's side of that line: the maw is behind him at screen-right (viewer-right, his left), the teammates off-screen up and to his right (viewer-left). Edges: his raised right hand at the frame's left edge, the maw and the rig at the right, sky above, sand below.

## 4. CHARACTER DIRECTION

- **Ziggy's acting note, verbatim (relayed by CFA Nova, 2026-10-14):** "Look over off-screen, UP TO YOUR RIGHT, and indicate to your teammates that it's concern."
- **Who:** Lisan, three-quarter to camera at screen-left, his right hand already raised palm-out at the frame's left edge; the breather on.
- **Eyeline (one target):** from about the half-second his eyes leave the camera (they are toward it in the still, a touch off the lens) and by about the first second and a half they are UP and to HIS RIGHT, which is VIEWER-LEFT, off-screen above the frame's left edge, past and above his raised hand, where his teammates stand on the higher dune behind the camera's left shoulder. The chin lifts a little; the head turns toward them by a few degrees; the shoulders stay square. The eyes stay on that one target for the whole take: no wander, no glance back at the worm, no return to the lens.
- **Face, the read is concern:** brows draw down and together; the eyes open a fraction wider as they find the teammates, then narrow a touch against the dust; the jaw sets; the lips, slightly parted in the start frame, press closed within the first half-second and stay closed. One hard breath through the nose as the rig goes under (the nostrils flare once); the breather's clip does not move. No words, no mouthing, no teeth.
- **Hands and body:** the raised right hand (viewer-left) is already a hold. Two small pushes of the open palm toward the teammates, up and to his right, each no more than a hand's width out and back: the first as the maw begins to close, the second smaller and sharper as the rig goes under; between and after, the hand open and still, five fingers spread, the forearm where it was. No fist, no wave, no pointing; the left hand stays out of frame; no step; no body turn.
- **Blinks:** one at most.
- **Mouth rule:** closes in the first half-second and stays closed; this is not a to-lens or sung shot.

## 5. BACKGROUND ACTION

Nothing else moves but dust and embers. Not allowed: a second worm, the maw turning toward him, anything reaching him, the fire spreading to him, people entering the frame.
