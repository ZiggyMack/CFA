## Story beat

The salt flat is silent (panel 09). He walks out onto the open sand with a thumper, drives it in, and lets it beat. The ground answers: tracks come toward him from the horizon, the sand lifts, and the maw rises out of the haze behind him. He does not run and he does not turn; he holds, one breath, eyes on us. Then the sand takes the frame, and he comes up out of it (panel 10). What the burst means, that the worm passed over and he surfaced, is the writer's reading, not a decision.

It is the right first clip for the same reason the ramp carry was on the Hon Dolo side: one character, one action, no mouth motion, no second figure, and the identity marks are chest-up in the hero frame. It is also the beat that makes the A-line (the desert) real: after it, every cut back to the desert carries the worm's possibility.

Why here: the Jet Black record asks for "an existing Lisan action that can serve as the first mission step." Planting the thumper is one (see the pass, section 3). That association is proposed, not placed.
