# The Curator (working name) - Story Bible
Role: the wise mentor of the saga (Gandalf-type). Old beyond stars, speaks in riddles, guides but rarely fights; when he does, it costs him (his nebula fades grey).

## Locations
- The Dune Sea: Lisan's homeland, worms beneath.
- Sietch of Echoes: cliff city where voices of the past linger.
- The Orrery Observatory: the Curator's home at the edge of space.
- The Glass Desert: sand fused to glass by an ancient star-fall.
- The Void Gate: a tear in the sky where celestial threats come through.

## Artifacts
- Shards of the Cube: pieces of his pocket universe scattered across the world.
- The Worm's Eye: a pearl from the First Worm; shows the future.
- The Star Compass: points to dying stars.
- The Sun-Disc Amulet: matches the eye-sun on Lisan's throat; unlocks his destiny.
- The Lantern of First Dawn: holds the first light ever made.

## Arcs (15 storyboard frames each)
1. The Falling Shard: a cube shard crashes into the Dune Sea; Lisan finds it; the Curator arrives; first lesson.
2. The Tomb of the First Worm: journey through the Glass Desert to claim the Worm's Eye.
3. The Star-Eater: a celestial devourer swallows stars; Star Compass; the Observatory.
4. The Eclipse Court: diplomacy with celestial beings; the Sun-Disc Amulet awakens Lisan.
5. The Last Light: battle at the Void Gate; the Curator gives his light; Lisan carries the Lantern; the Curator returns reborn.
