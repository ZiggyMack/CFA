## Story beat

Verse 3 has gone all the way inward: the library, the masks, the spiral, the shadow, the voodoo, the chaos cards. Then the desert again, and two doors standing in the open sand with nothing behind them: through one, the sun; through the other, the stars. He stands between them. The song has been two worlds the whole way, the desert where his body is and the mirror where he is everyone, and this is the frame where the film says so out loud. He goes toward the sun. Street level, a car hood, a crowd, the mic to the lens: not a throne, a hood. And then the blade up and the mandala blazing over the desert, the mirror's sun in the desert's sky, and inside the mandala an eye, the same eye that has been on his throat since the first frame. The signal was never out there. He was carrying it.
